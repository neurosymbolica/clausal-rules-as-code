# ind/dpdp_notice_consent

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ss 2(g), 2(i), 4, 5, 6 and 7, with the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 rules 1(4), 3, 4 and 5 and the First and Second Schedules. Decided here, one question per query: whether personal data may be processed on the ground of consent (s 4(1)(a), s 4(2), s 6(1), s 6(4)); whether it may be processed for a listed legitimate use (s 4(1)(b), s 7, one clause per question); which notice the Data Fiduciary owes (s 5(1), s 5(2)(a), s 5(3), with rule 3 from 13 May 2027 on the calculated date of rule 1(4)); what a withdrawal of consent requires of the Data Fiduciary (s 6(4) to (6)); whether a person may act as a Consent Manager (s 2(g), s 6(7) to (9), rule 4(2)(a), (3) and (5)(a)); and the fixed lists of notice contents, request requirements, legitimate uses, Second Schedule standards and Consent Manager conditions and obligations. Out of scope: when the Act's sections came into force (caller facts); the open-textured judgements (lawful purpose, the quality of consent under s 6(1), the conditions of a s 7 clause, Data Fiduciary status, processing required or authorised by law), which are handed to a lawyer; the Data Principal's rights (ss ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 246 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Every citation record quotes its statute word for word against the pinned text under `sources/` (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
