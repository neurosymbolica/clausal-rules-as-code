# ind/dpdp_appeal_information

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Two procedures of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 with the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025 (drafter's reading, not a lawyer's). (a) The appeal to the Appellate Tribunal against an order or direction of the Data Protection Board of India (s 29(1) to (3), (6) and (7) with rule 22(1) and (2)): whether an appeal lies and is filed within the sixty days of s 29(2) (appeal_period, a late appeal answered as needing sufficient cause, never as barred), what the filing must meet under rule 22 (appeal_filing_requirements) and the six-month target of s 29(6) with the duty of s 29(7) to record reasons (appeal_disposal_target). (b) The Central Government's call for information (s 36 with rule 23(1) and (2) and the Seventh Schedule): whether the recipient must furnish it (information_call_status) and whether rule 23(2) bars disclosure of the furnishing (non_disclosure_status). Rule 1(4) dates rules 22 and 23 (13 May 2027, a calculated date). The commencement of s 29 and s 36, the open-textured judgements and the identity of an intermediary or authorised person are stated by the caller; a call outside the Seventh Schedule and every unsure judgement go to a lawyer. ## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 216 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Every citation record quotes its statute word for word against the pinned text under `sources/` (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
