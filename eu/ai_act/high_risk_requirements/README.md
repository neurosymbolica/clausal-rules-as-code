# eu/ai_act/high_risk_requirements

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act), consolidated text 02024R1689-20260727, Chapter III, Section 2. Decided here: whether, on an assessment day, each of nine requirements (risk management system Art 9(1); data and data governance Art 10(1) and (6); technical documentation Art 11(1), with the simplified form for SMEs and SMCs; record-keeping Art 12(1); minimum logging capabilities Art 12(3); transparency and instructions for use Art 13(1) and (2); human oversight Art 14(1); separate verification by two natural persons Art 14(5); accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity Art 15(1)) applies to one AI system through Art 8(1), under the application dates of Art 113, third paragraph, point (c), and the transitional rules of Art 111(1) and (2); and whether each element of Annex IV, Art 11(2), Art 13(3) and Art 15(3) is required. Out of scope: whether the system meets a requirement (open words), the scope of the Regulation (Art 2), the classification (Art 6, Annexes I and III), the provider duties (Art 16), Art 4a and Annex X membership; the open judgements significant changes in design and intended use by public authorities are stated by the caller and, when unsure, ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 194 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Every citation record quotes its statute word for word against the pinned text under `sources/` (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
