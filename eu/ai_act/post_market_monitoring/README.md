# eu/ai_act/post_market_monitoring

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act), consolidated text 02024R1689-20260727. Decided here: whether, on a given assessment date, the actor named as provider of one AI system must establish and document a post-market monitoring system based on a post-market monitoring plan that is part of the technical documentation (Art 72(1) and 72(3), first and second sentences), judged at the Chapter III application dates of Art 113, third paragraph, point (c)(i) and (ii), with the transitional rules for Annex X large-scale IT system components (Art 111(1)) and for systems already placed on the market or put into service (Art 111(2)); and whether that provider has the choice of Art 72(4) to integrate into a post-market monitoring system and plan already established under Union harmonisation legislation of Annex I Section A or Union financial services law. Out of scope: scope of the Regulation (Art 2), the role of provider (Art 3(3), order scope-and-roles), high-risk classification (Art 6, Annexes I and III), what the system collects and analyses (Art 72(2)), proportionality, the technical documentation (Art 11, Annex IV), the Commission template, and serious-incident reporting ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.
