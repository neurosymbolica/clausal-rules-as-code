# eu/ai_act/small_items

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act), consolidated text 02024R1689-20260727 (as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744): six bounded questions, each the cumulative conditions of one Article on the assessment date: whether Art 4a(1) or (2) permits the processing of special categories of personal data for bias detection and correction; whether Art 59(1) and (2) permit the further processing of personal data in an AI regulatory sandbox; whether Art 60(1) and (4) permit testing of a high-risk AI system in real world conditions outside a sandbox, or Art 60a(1), (2) and (5) leave it to a Member State framework; whether a consent meets Art 61(1) and (2); whether Art 63(1) and (2) let an SME comply with elements of the quality management system in a simplified manner; whether a person has the right to explanation of Art 86(1) to (3). Application dates from Art 113 (first paragraph, third paragraph point (a)). Open-textured judgements are handed to the user or to a lawyer (needs_legal_review), never decided. Out of scope: the scope order of Art 2, the data protection acts, national law, the classification as high-risk (Art 6, Annexes I and III), Art 10 and Art 17, the ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 300 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Every citation record quotes its statute word for word against the pinned text under `sources/` (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
