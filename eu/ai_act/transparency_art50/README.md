# eu/ai_act/transparency_art50

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Whether, on a calendar day the caller gives, one of the five transparency duties of Article 50(1) to (4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act), in the consolidated text 02024R1689-20260727 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, applies to one system the caller describes (interaction information, machine-readable marking, emotion recognition or biometric categorisation information, deep-fake disclosure, public-interest text disclosure), read with Article 3, points (1) and (4), the date of application of Article 113, second sentence (2 August 2026), and the transition of Article 111(4) for marking; and whether that duty is met under Article 50(5). Open-textured judgements (obviousness, assistive editing, substantial alteration, emotion recognition or biometric categorisation system, deep fake, public-interest text, artistic work, appropriate disclosure, clarity, accessibility, marking, AI system) are given by the caller and, when unsure and decisive, referred to a lawyer. Out of scope: who the provider or deployer is, whether the Regulation applies at all (Article 2), the design and development limb of Article 50(1), the technical-solutions sentence of Article ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.
