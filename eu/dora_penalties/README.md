# eu/dora_penalties

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA), Articles 50 to 52, for one breach of the Regulation by a financial entity, from the pinned original OJ text 32022R2554. Given the national choices and facts the caller states, the order answers five questions: whether administrative penalty rules are required for the breach (Art 50(3) with the option of Art 52(1)); the minimum supervisory and investigatory powers (Art 50(2)) and administrative penalties and remedial measures (Art 50(4)) the competent authorities must have, with the extension of Art 50(5); the safeguards of Art 50(6) for a decision imposing measures of Art 50(2) point (c); the circumstances Art 51(2) requires the authority to take into account; and the liaison duties of Art 52(2). Out of scope: the general clause of Art 50(1), the quality of the penalties (Art 50(3) second sentence), the ways of exercise (Art 51(1)), the conditions national law sets, and Articles 35(6), 53 and 54; an unsure Art 51(2) appraisal is handed to a lawyer. Every reading is the drafter's, not a lawyer's.

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 182 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Every citation record quotes its statute word for word against the pinned text under `sources/` (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
