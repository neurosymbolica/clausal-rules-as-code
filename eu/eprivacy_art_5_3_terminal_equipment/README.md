# eu/eprivacy_art_5_3_terminal_equipment

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Directive 2002/58/EC (ePrivacy Directive) Article 5(3), first and second sentences, consolidated text 02002L0058-20091219 (as amended by Directive 2009/136/EC), read with Article 2, second paragraph, point (f) (consent) and point (a) (user, context only). Decided here: whether the storing of information, or the gaining of access to information already stored, in the terminal equipment of a subscriber or user is permitted, not permitted or outside Article 5(3), as the Directive states what the Member States shall ensure: permitted when any one of three ways is met (the transmission exemption; the strictly necessary exemption for an explicitly requested information society service; consent given after clear and comprehensive information), not permitted when all three fail. Every open-textured point (scope, sole purpose, explicit request, strict necessity, valid consent, clear and comprehensive information) is a judgement the caller states; an unsure judgement goes to a lawyer (needs_legal_review), a missing key is asked for (indeterminate). Out of scope: any Member State's transposing law, the conditions of valid consent under Directive 95/46/EC and Regulation (EU) ...
