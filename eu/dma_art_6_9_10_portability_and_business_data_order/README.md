# eu/dma_art_6_9_10_portability_and_business_data_order

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2022/1925 (Digital Markets Act), Art 6(1), 6(9) and 6(10), with Art 2(25) and 2(32). For one profile (one gatekeeper, one core platform service, one request by one requester, one set of requested data) the order decides three status questions with a cited answer: whether the obligations of Article 6 apply to the service (it is listed in a Commission designation decision, an attested outside fact); whether the gatekeeper meets the duty of Art 6(9) to provide effective portability of end user data, free of charge, with tools and continuous and real-time access, on request; and whether it meets the duty of Art 6(10) to provide a business user or a third party it authorised with free, effective, high-quality, continuous and real-time access to and use of data, with the personal-data limits of the second subparagraph (direct connection and end user opt-in consent, the consent an attested outside fact). Open-textured judgements are the caller's (true, false or unsure) and an unsure judgement that decides is referred to a lawyer. Out of scope: the other paragraphs of Article 6, Article 5, Article 3 (designation, compliance period), Article 8 measures, Regulation (EU) ...
