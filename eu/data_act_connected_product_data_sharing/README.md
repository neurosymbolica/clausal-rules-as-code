# eu/data_act_connected_product_data_sharing

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 (the Data Act), Chapter II: whether the design duty of Art 3(1) is met, whether the pre-contract information duty of Art 3(2) (purchase, rent or lease of a connected product) or Art 3(3) (provision of a related service) is met, whether a data holder's duty to make readily available data accessible to the user arises under Art 4 (with the security restriction of Art 4(2), the personal-data bar of Art 4(12) and the trade-secret grounds of Art 4(6) to 4(8)), and whether its duty to share such data with a third party arises under Art 5 (with the testing-data exception of Art 5(2), the gatekeeper exclusion of Art 5(3), the personal-data bar of Art 5(7) and the trade-secret grounds of Art 5(9) to 5(11)); read with the Art 2 definitions (5), (6), (12), (13), (15), (16) and (17), the small enterprise exemption of Art 7(1) and the application dates of Art 50, second and third paragraphs. An answer says whether a duty arises or a ground applies, not whether data were made available in the right manner or in time. Out of scope: Chapter III onward (fair terms, compensation, dispute settlement, authorities), penalties (Art 40), national law, the size classes of ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.
