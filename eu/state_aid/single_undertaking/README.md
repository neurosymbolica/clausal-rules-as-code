# eu/state_aid/single_undertaking

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/2831 (de minimis aid), Art 2(2): the definition of a single undertaking, its lead-in, points (a) to (d) and its closing sentence. Three questions are decided: whether one enterprise has a relationship of points (a) to (d) over another (direct_relationship/3: majority of voting rights, tested exactly as 2 x held greater than total; the right to appoint or remove a majority of the body; the right to dominant influence by contract or articles; sole control of a majority of voting rights by agreement); whether two named enterprises belong to one single undertaking on the caller's list of relationships, directly or through one or more other enterprises (same_single_undertaking/3); and which enterprises form the single undertaking of one named enterprise (single_undertaking_members/4). Open-textured judgements (whether an entity is an enterprise, dominant influence, an unsure relationship) are handed to a lawyer as needs_legal_review; a missing fact is asked for as indeterminate. Out of scope: the ceiling of Art 3(2), amounts and the period of 3 years, the count per Member State, mergers and splits, the scope and sector exclusions of Art 1, the dates of ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.
