# eu/dora_rts_simplified_policies_and_controls

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

For ONE financial entity, which duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework in Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1774 (the RTS), Title III, Articles 28 to 35 (governance and organisation, the information security policy and ICT security measures, classification of functions and assets, ICT risk management, physical and environmental security, access control, ICT operations security, data, system and network security) apply to it (simplified_policies_and_controls_duties_required), whether it has performed all of them (simplified_policies_and_controls_duties_complete) and which are outstanding (simplified_policies_and_controls_duties_outstanding). The gate is Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA), Article 16(1), first subparagraph, stated by the caller as a crisp fact. Each duty is a tri-state caller judgement; an unsure judgement is referred to a lawyer as open_textured, never decided by a crisp test. Out of scope: whether the entity is a financial entity within the scope of DORA and which entities are those of Article 16(1) (DORA orders on scope and on proportionality); the duties of Article 16(1), points (a) to (h), and Article 16(2) of DORA itself; Articles 36 to ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 189 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Every citation record quotes its statute word for word against the pinned text under `sources/` (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
