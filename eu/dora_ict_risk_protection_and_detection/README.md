# eu/dora_ict_risk_protection_and_detection

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

For ONE financial entity presupposed to be within the scope of Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA): which of the duties of protection and prevention (Article 9) and of detection (Article 10) apply to it, whether it has performed all of them, and which are outstanding, with the rule of Article 16(1), first subparagraph, that Articles 5 to 15 do not apply to the entities it lists (answered simplified_framework_entity). Each duty is a caller judgement (true, false, unsure); an unsure judgement that matters is referred to a lawyer. Out of scope: whether the entity is a financial entity within scope (the order on scope and entity types), the simplified ICT risk management framework of Article 16(1), second subparagraph, (2) and (3) (the order on proportionality and the simplified framework), Articles 5 to 8 and 11 to 15 (their own orders), Articles 17 and 25, and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1774. Every reading is the drafter's reading, not a lawyer's.

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked
