# eu/dora_rts_change_management

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

The financial entity's duties of ICT change management under Article 17(1) and (2) of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1774 (RTS on ICT risk management under Article 15 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, DORA), with the rule of DORA Article 16(1), first subparagraph, that Articles 5 to 15 of DORA do not apply to the entities it lists (answered simplified_framework_entity). For ONE financial entity it decides which of the sixteen duties apply (thirteen elements of Art 17(1), always; the stringent testing of Art 17(2) and the involvement of parties, for central counterparties and central securities depositories after significant changes), whether all are performed as the caller judges, and which are outstanding. Out of scope: whether the entity is a financial entity within DORA (presupposed), the simplified framework of DORA Art 16 and Title III, DORA Art 9(4)(e) itself, the other articles of the Delegated Regulation, and every open-textured judgement (adequate, effective, stringent, as appropriate), which is the caller's or, when unsure, a lawyer's.

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.
