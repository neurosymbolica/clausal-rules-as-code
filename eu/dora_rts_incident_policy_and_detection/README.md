# eu/dora_rts_incident_policy_and_detection

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

For ONE financial entity presupposed to be within the scope of DORA, the duties of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1774 Article 22 (the ICT-related incident management policy: the policy itself, points (a) to (e) and the secure retention of evidence) and Article 23 (roles and responsibilities, the detection mechanism of paragraph 2 points (a) to (d) and its automated alerts on data source and log integrity, protection of recordings, logging of each detected anomalous activity, the criteria to trigger detection and response, and the criticality of the services affected): which of the seventeen duties apply, whether all are performed, and which are outstanding, each performance being a caller judgement (true, false or unsure; an unsure judgement is referred to a lawyer as open_textured). The only relief is Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 Article 16(1), first subparagraph: an entity listed there answers simplified_framework_entity. Out of scope: whether the entity is a financial entity in scope of DORA, the simplified ICT risk management framework (DORA Art 16(1) second subparagraph, 16(2), 16(3) and RTS Title III), DORA Articles 10 and 17, RTS Article 12, Delegated Regulation ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.
