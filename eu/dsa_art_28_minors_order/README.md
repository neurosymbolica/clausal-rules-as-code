# eu/dsa_art_28_minors_order

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

For one provider of one online platform service and, for Art 28(2), one practice of presenting advertisements to one recipient (or recipients treated alike), this domain answers four questions under Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act), original act, English text: (1) whether Section 3 of Chapter III applies to the provider under the micro and small enterprise exclusion of Art 19(1) and its derogation for designated very large online platforms in Art 19(2); (2) whether the obligation of Art 28(1) to put in place appropriate and proportionate measures for a high level of privacy, safety and security of minors applies; (3) whether the provider meets that obligation; (4) whether presenting advertisements based on profiling of a recipient the provider is aware with reasonable certainty is a minor is prohibited by Art 28(2). Out of scope and left to the caller or a lawyer: the open-textured judgements (accessibility to minors, appropriateness and proportionality of the measures, profiling, reasonable-certainty awareness), the enterprise findings under Recommendation 2003/361/EC, the Commission designation under Art 33 (an attested outside fact), the definitions of Art 3, ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.
