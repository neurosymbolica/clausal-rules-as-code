# eu/nis2_supervision_fines

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS 2), Arts 32(2), 32(4), 32(5), 33(2), 33(4), 34(4), 34(5) and 35(1) to (3): for an essential or important entity, (A) the least national maximum fine a Member State must provide for an infringement of Article 21 or 23 (a floor of the national ceiling, never a fine; no Union minimum for any other provision); (B) the minimum supervisory, enforcement and escalation powers a Member State must give its competent authorities, in article order, without the Art 32(5) powers for a public administration entity; (C) whether the Art 32(5) escalation powers are exercisable (four conditions; the eligibility verdict of the facade, with a cited checklist); (D) whether a GDPR Art 58(2)(i) fine for the same conduct bars the NIS2 fine (Art 35(2)); (E) the duty to inform the data protection supervisory authorities (Art 35(1), (3)). Open-textured judgements (ineffective earlier measures, a possible notifiable personal data breach, the same conduct) are handed to the user or a lawyer, never decided by a crisp test. Out of scope: the actual national maximum and penalties (Art 36, country orders), the choice of measure or fine amount (Art 32(7) factors), entity classification ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 191 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Every citation record quotes its statute word for word against the pinned text under `sources/` (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
