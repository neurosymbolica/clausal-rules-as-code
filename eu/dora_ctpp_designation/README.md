# eu/dora_ctpp_designation

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Article 31 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA), read with its Articles 2 and 3 for the categories of financial entities and the definitions the readings name, and Articles 1 to 3 and 5 of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1502 (THE CRITERIA REGULATION). For ONE undertaking providing ICT services (THE PROVIDER) it decides: whether Article 31(8) excludes THE PROVIDER from designation as a critical ICT third-party service provider and otherwise whether it fulfils every 'step 1' sub-criterion, so that the ESAs go on to the 'step 2' assessment (ctpp_designation_criteria_status/3); which 'step 1' sub-criteria the stated figures fulfil, and in which categories of financial entities (ctpp_step_1_indicators/3); which ESA Article 31(1), point (b), appoints as Lead Overseer, or that the largest sums are tied (ctpp_lead_overseer/3); and which duties Article 31(4), the last sentence of Article 31(5) and Article 31(13) place on a designated provider (ctpp_designated_provider_duties/3). Out of scope (see deferred_set and excluded_set): the 'step 2' assessment, the designation itself, the notifications, reasoned statement and periods of the authorities' procedure, the list of critical ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.
