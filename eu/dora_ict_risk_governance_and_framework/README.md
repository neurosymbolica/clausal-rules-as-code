# eu/dora_ict_risk_governance_and_framework

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

The ICT risk governance and framework duties of ONE financial entity under Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA): the internal governance and control framework and the duties of the management body (Article 5), the ICT risk management framework, its control function, review, internal audit, follow-up and the digital operational resilience strategy (Article 6(1) to (8)), the ICT systems, protocols and tools (Article 7) and the identification of functions, assets, sources of ICT risk and dependencies (Article 8), with Article 16(1), first subparagraph, read only for the rule that Articles 5 to 15 do not apply to the entities it lists. Three questions: which of the thirty-four duties apply (ict_risk_governance_duties_required), whether all are performed (ict_risk_governance_duties_complete), and which are outstanding (ict_risk_governance_duties_outstanding). Out of scope: whether the entity is a financial entity in scope and of which type (the order on DORA scope and entity types; the caller states the Article 16(1) and microenterprise facts), the options of Article 6(9) and (10), the simplified framework of Article 16, the proportionality principle of Article 4, Articles 9 to 15, Chapter ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.
