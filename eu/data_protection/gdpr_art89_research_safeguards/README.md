# eu/data_protection/gdpr_art89_research_safeguards

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) Art 89(1): whether processing for archiving in the public interest, scientific or historical research, or statistical purposes is subject to appropriate safeguards. Decides four requirements (technical and organisational measures, minimisation measures, the identification requirement of the fourth sentence, and appropriateness of the safeguards) by strong Kleene logic, with the two judgements (non_identifying_processing_sufficient, safeguards_appropriate) handed to the user as needs_legal_review. Pseudonymisation (third sentence) permits and requires nothing and is not read. Out of scope: the derogations of Art 89(2) to (4) (Union or Member State law), the lawfulness basis of the processing, and any purpose other than the four named ones (reported as not_applicable).

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.
