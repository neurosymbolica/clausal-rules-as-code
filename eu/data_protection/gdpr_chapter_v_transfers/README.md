# eu/data_protection/gdpr_chapter_v_transfers

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), Chapter V, Articles 44, 45(1), 45(3), 45(8), 46(1), 46(2)(b), 47(1), 48 and 49(1) to 49(4). Decided here: given a transfer profile, whether a transfer of personal data to a third country or an international organisation may take place and on which basis of the cascade (Art 45 adequacy decision as an attested outside fact, then Art 46 appropriate safeguards including approved binding corporate rules, then the claimed Art 49 derogation with the subsidiarity of the second subparagraph, the Art 49(3) public-authority carve-out and the Art 49(2) register limits). Open-textured judgements are handed to the user and referred to a lawyer when unsure. Out of scope: the Art 6 lawful basis (sibling gdpr-art6-lawfulness), the content of adequacy decisions and of Art 46(2) and (3) instruments, the content of binding corporate rules under Art 47(2), recognition of third-country judgments under Art 48 (recorded only), onward transfers as separate assessments (recorded only), and Art 49(5) and (6) (see deferred_set).

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 201 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Every citation record quotes its statute word for word against the pinned text under `sources/` (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
