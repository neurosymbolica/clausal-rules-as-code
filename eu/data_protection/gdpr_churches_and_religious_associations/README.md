# eu/data_protection/gdpr_churches_and_religious_associations

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Article 91 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), from the pinned text only. church_rules_continuation/4 answers whether Art 91(1) lets the comprehensive rules a church or a religious association or community applied in a Member State at the time of entry into force of the Regulation continue to apply, provided that they are brought into line with the Regulation. church_supervision/4 answers, for the same profile, whether Art 91(2) is engaged and, if it is, whether the church or association is subject to an independent supervisory authority, or whether a specific authority is permitted (it is attested to fulfil the conditions of Chapter VI) or not. Out of scope and handed to the caller or a lawyer: the content of any church rule or Member State law; whether the rules are comprehensive and brought into line (tri-state judgements, unsure refers to a lawyer); the time of entry into force (Art 99) and the conditions of Chapter VI, both taken only as attested outside facts; the definitions of Art 4.

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 175 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Every citation record quotes its statute word for word against the pinned text under `sources/` (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
