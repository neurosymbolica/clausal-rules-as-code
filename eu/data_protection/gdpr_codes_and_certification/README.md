# eu/data_protection/gdpr_codes_and_certification

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) Articles 40 to 43, from the pinned text of the consolidated version of 4 May 2016 only, as rules over facts the caller states. certification_status/5: for one certification at a CHECK_DATE, whether the conditions of Articles 42(5), 42(7) and 43(1) on its issuer, its approved criteria, informing the supervisory authority, the maximum period of three years and withdrawal hold, and, answered on its own, whether the conditions of Article 42(2) for its use in transfers hold. code_monitoring_status/3: for one code of conduct, whether its compliance is monitored by an accredited body as Articles 40(2), 40(4), 40(5), 41(1) and 41(6) state, naming the two duties Article 41(4) gives that body, and, answered on its own, whether the conditions of Articles 40(3) and 40(9) for its use in transfers hold. accreditation_status/6: for one monitoring body (Articles 41(2) and 41(5)) or one certification body (Articles 43(1), 43(2), 43(4) and 43(7), maximum period of five years) at a CHECK_DATE, whether the conditions the text states for its accreditation hold. Out of scope: whether processing complies with the Regulation, whether a code or a certification is good or ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 338 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Every citation record quotes its statute word for word against the pinned text under `sources/` (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
