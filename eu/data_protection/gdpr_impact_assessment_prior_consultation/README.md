# eu/data_protection/gdpr_impact_assessment_prior_consultation

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) Art 36(1) and Art 36(5), split order SPLIT 2026-11-03 of eu/data-protection/gdpr-impact-assessment: whether the controller must consult the supervisory authority prior to processing (decide_prior_consultation/2, prior_consultation_duty/2), on the residual-risk reading of Art 36(1) (Q1 RULED 2026-11-01, WP29 WP248 rev.01 section III.E) or because attested Member State law under Art 36(5) requires consultation and prior authorisation for public-interest processing; plus the outside facts as given (impact_assessment_outside_facts/2) and the typed input check of the original order. The high-risk and residual-risk judgements are the user's. Out of scope: whether an impact assessment is owed under Art 35 (a missing assessment is referred, not_governed_here, Q5), its bases, contents and proof routes (the sibling split orders), the Art 36(2) authority periods, the Art 36(3) request contents, Art 36(4), and the content of Member State law or supervisory-authority lists (attested outside facts, fetcher none).

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.
