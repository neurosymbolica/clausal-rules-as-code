# eu/data_protection/gdpr_transfer_dpf

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795 (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework) Article 1, read with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) Article 45(1). The bounded question: does Decision (EU) 2023/1795 cover THIS transfer of personal data to an organisation in the United States, so that Art 45(1) GDPR lets it take place without any specific authorisation? Decided here, as one limb (data_privacy_framework_adequacy/4): five conditions that must all hold, each deciding by itself when it fails -- the data are transferred from the Union and the recipient is an organisation in the United States (crisp caller facts); the decision is in force for the transfer, the recipient is on the Data Privacy Framework List, and the data fall within what its certification covers (attested outside facts, never decided here). An absent outside fact is referred before a missing crisp key is named. Out of scope, quoted or deferred: Articles 2 to 4 of the decision (Member State information, monitoring, review, suspension, amendment, repeal, addressees), the recitals, the footnotes and the Annexes (the Principles, the List mechanics, the human-resources and journalistic limits, the U.S. letters); the Chapter ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 175 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Every citation record quotes its statute word for word against the pinned text under `sources/` (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
