# eu/data_protection/gdpr_records_and_officer

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), three entry points over one attribute-list profile. ENTRY 1: whether a controller (Art 30(1)) or a processor (Art 30(2)), or both, owes a record of processing activities for ONE processing activity, given the Art 30(5) exemption below 250 persons employed and its four triggers (a risk, not occasional, special categories, criminal offence data), and which content items of Art 30(1)(a)-(g) and Art 30(2)(a)-(d) the record lacks. ENTRY 2: whether a data protection officer must be designated under the three limbs of Art 37(1) or because Union or Member State law requires it (Art 37(4), an attested outside fact), and which of the Art 39(1) tasks the officer's mandate lacks. ENTRY 3: whether the position of the officer meets one duty of Art 38(1), (2), (3) or (6), each read from an attested outside fact or, for the conflict of interests, the user's judgement. Open-textured terms (risk, occasional, core activity, large scale, where possible, conflict of interests) are the user's judgements and an unsure one is referred to a lawyer; whether an entity is a public authority or body and the content of national law are outside facts. ## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 200 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Every citation record quotes its statute word for word against the pinned text under `sources/` (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
