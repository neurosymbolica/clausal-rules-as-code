# eu/data_protection/gdpr_accountability_security

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) Art 5(2), 24(1) and (2), 25(1) and (2), 26(1) and (2), 32(1), (2) and (4): for the caller (controller or processor, as given), whether each of seven accountability and security duties is met, read from one attested outside statement per duty (Art 25(2) also from five optional keys that can only give not_met); whether joint controllers need and have an arrangement (Art 26(1)); whether the arrangement meets its content requirements (Art 26(2)); and the fixed lists of non-binding items of Art 5(1)(a)-(f) and Art 32(1)(a)-(d). The order never decides 'appropriate' or 'jointly determine': those judgements are handed to the user (attested statements, the unsure value) or to a lawyer. Out of scope: roles (sibling order on definitions and roles), lawfulness and the other Art 5(1) principles (sibling orders), codes of conduct and certification (Art 24(3), 25(3), 32(3), deferred), the data subject's rights against each joint controller (Art 26(3), deferred), liability allocation.

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 199 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Every citation record quotes its statute word for word against the pinned text under `sources/` (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
