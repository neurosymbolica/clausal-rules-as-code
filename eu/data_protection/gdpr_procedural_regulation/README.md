# eu/data_protection/gdpr_procedural_regulation

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2025/2518 (GDPR Procedural Regulation), for a complaint or an investigation concerning cross-border processing (the caller establishes the cross-border character and the roles of the authorities). Decided here, from the pinned text only: the admissibility of a complaint (Art 4(1), 4(2), 4(5)); whether the prior request to the controller of Art 4(3) is required and made (reported, not made an item of admissibility); what follows from an early resolution (Art 5(1) to (4) and (6)); whether the simple cooperation procedure is available (Art 6(1), 6(3), 6(5)); which time limit applies to the draft decision and whether its extension is permitted (Art 6(1), Art 12(1) to (3) and (6)); the effect of a procedural step taken late (Art 14); a table of sixteen time limits (Arts 4, 5, 6, 10 and 12) stated as a number, a unit, a relation, an event and any extension; and from when Chapters II to VI apply (Art 36, Art 37(2)). ## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
