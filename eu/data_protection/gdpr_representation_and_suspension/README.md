# eu/data_protection/gdpr_representation_and_suspension

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) Art 80 (representation of data subjects) and Art 81 (suspension of proceedings), from the pinned text of 02016R0679-20160504 only. Decided here: whether a not-for-profit body, organisation or association may act on a data subject mandate for one of the four actions Art 80(1) names (mandated_representation); whether such a body may act without a mandate where a Member State has so provided (representation_without_mandate, Art 80(2)); whether a competent court must contact the court of another Member State (court_contact_duty, Art 81(1)); whether a court other than the court first seized may suspend its proceedings (court_suspension_option, Art 81(2)) or decline jurisdiction (court_declining_jurisdiction_option, Art 81(3)). Out of scope: whether the data subject has the rights of Arts 77, 78, 79 or 82 (sibling orders gdpr-complaints-and-judicial-remedies and gdpr-compensation-and-liability); the law of the Member States on the constitution of bodies, mandates, compensation claims, competence, seising, jurisdiction and consolidation, read only as attested outside facts or caller statements and never decided; how a court should use its options.
