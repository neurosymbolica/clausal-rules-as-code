# eu/data_protection/gdpr_common_definitions_roles_and_establishments

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) Art 4, points (7), (8), (13), (14), (15), (16), (18), (19), (22), (23) and (24): given one profile of facts about one situation, whether each defined term is met (controller, processor, genetic data, biometric data, data concerning health, main establishment, enterprise, group of undertakings, supervisory authority concerned, cross-border processing, relevant and reasoned objection), with personal data (Art 4(1)) carried as one judgement and the one outside fact of Art 4(7) (law providing for the controller) read as an attested value. Open-textured judgements are the user's; an unsure judgement or an absent outside fact that decides is referred for legal review. Out of scope: whether any obligation applies or which supervisory authority leads; points 4(17), 4(20) and 4(21) (deferred; 4(20) in the sibling order gdpr-binding-corporate-rules) and the other points of Art 4 (sibling order gdpr-common-definitions-data-and-processing).

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.
