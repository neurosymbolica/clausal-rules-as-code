# eu/data_protection/gdpr_binding_corporate_rules

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), consolidated text 02016R0679-20160504, Art 4(20) and Art 47(2), points (a) to (n). Given a profile of one set of policies, the party that adheres to them and the transfers they are for, the package answers: (A) whether the policies are binding corporate rules within the meaning of Art 4(20), read as seven elements (binding_corporate_rules_definition/3); (B) whether the rules specify everything Art 47(2) says binding corporate rules shall specify at least, the whole of each point, presence not adequacy except point (n) (binding_corporate_rules_content/3); (C) the state of each point (a) to (n) with its citation (binding_corporate_rules_content_checklist/2). The roles of controller and processor, the group element and the appropriateness of training are the caller's judgements; an unsure judgement goes to a lawyer (needs_legal_review). OUT OF SCOPE: the approval of binding corporate rules by the competent supervisory authority under Art 47(1) (an act of the authority; read by gdpr-chapter-v-transfers as binding_corporate_rules_approved), whether a transfer may take place (Chapter V, gdpr-chapter-v-transfers), whether the group applies its rules in ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 214 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Every citation record quotes its statute word for word against the pinned text under `sources/` (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
