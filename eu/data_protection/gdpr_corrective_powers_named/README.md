# eu/data_protection/gdpr_corrective_powers_named

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) Article 58(2), the chapeau and points (a) to (j), pinned unit text of the consolidated text of 4 May 2016. Decided here: whether the text NAMES one given corrective power for a stated situation and against which addressees (corrective_power_named/4), and which of the ten powers it names, in the order of the points (corrective_powers_named/3). Every final answer carries authority_discretion: the text states no rule of choice among the powers and no duty to use one, and this order decides neither. The five legal conclusions (intended processing likely to infringe, processing that has infringed, processing not in compliance, a ground under Articles 16 to 18, certification requirements not met) are tri-state judgements the caller states; unsure refers to a lawyer. Out of scope: whether there is an infringement, which authority is competent, Article 58(1) and (3), the safeguards of Article 58(4), Member State powers under Article 58(5) and (6), Articles 16 to 19, 33 and 34, 42 and 43, Article 83 (gdpr-administrative-fine-maximum) and Chapter V (gdpr-chapter-v-transfers). No date and no amount is computed.

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 156 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Every citation record quotes its statute word for word against the pinned text under `sources/` (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
