# eu/eprivacy_breach_traffic_location_marketing

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Under the text of Directive 2002/58/EC (the ePrivacy Directive), consolidated text 02002L0058-20091219, only (no Member State's transposing law is read): which notifications of a personal data breach are due, to the competent national authority only or also to the subscriber or individual (Art 4(3), with Art 2(i)); whether one processing of traffic data for one purpose is permitted (Art 6(1) to (3), with Art 2(b), (f) and (g)); whether one processing of location data other than traffic data is permitted (Art 9(1) and (2), with Art 2(c) and (f)); whether one direct marketing communication may be sent, may not be sent, or is left to national law (Art 13(1), (2), (3) and (5), with Art 2(f) and (h)); and whether a direct marketing electronic mail practice is prohibited (Art 13(4)). Not decided here: the security duties of Art 4(1), (1a) and (2), Art 4(4) and (5), Art 6(4) to (6), Art 9(3), Art 13(6), the content and timing of notifications, and the transposing laws of the Member States. Every open-textured judgement is the caller's; an unsure judgement that matters is referred to a lawyer.

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.
