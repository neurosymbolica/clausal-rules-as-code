## Scope

Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS 2) Arts 29(1), 29(2), 29(4) and 30(1): what the Directive requires a Member State to ensure (national law says what an entity must do in fact). Three questions are decided: whether a voluntary notification of an incident, significant incident, cyber threat or near miss can be submitted under Art 30(1) (point (a) essential and important entities, any event; point (b) every other entity, significant incidents, cyber threats and near misses only); whether a voluntary exchange of cybersecurity information is one the Member States must let entities carry out under Art 29(1) (relevant information, and point (a) or point (b) read as alternatives); and whether Art 29(4) requires an essential or important entity to notify the competent authorities on entering, or on the withdrawal from, an information-sharing arrangement of Art 29(2). The open-textured judgements (relevance, the aims of points (a) and (b), whether an arrangement is one of Art 29(2)) are the caller's: an unsure judgement that decides is referred as needs_legal_review.
