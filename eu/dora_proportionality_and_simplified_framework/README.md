# eu/dora_proportionality_and_simplified_framework

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (Digital Operational Resilience Act, DORA), pinned original OJ text 32022R2554 (OJ L 333, 27.12.2022, p. 1). For ONE entity of ONE entity type this order decides: whether the entity is one of those referred to in Article 16(1), first subparagraph, to which Articles 5 to 15 do not apply (simplified_framework_qualification/3, reading Art 2(1)(u), Art 2(2), Art 2(3)(c), Art 2(4), first sentence, and Article 3, points (32), (34), (36), (39) and (53)); which duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework of Art 16(1), second subparagraph, points (a) to (h) (point (h) as two duties), and Art 16(2) apply to it (simplified_framework_duties_required/3); whether it has performed all of them (simplified_framework_duties_complete/3, the compliance check); and which of them are outstanding (simplified_framework_duties_outstanding/3). The principle of proportionality of Art 4(1) is read inside the caller's judgement of each duty and removes no duty. This order gives the rule for the gate key financial_entity_is_article_16_1_entity of the other DORA orders. ## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.
