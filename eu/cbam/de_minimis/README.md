# eu/cbam/de_minimis

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2023/956 (CBAM), consolidated as amended to 2025-10-20, Art 2a(1), (2) and (4), read with Art 2(1), Art 3 point (15), Annex I point 1 and its Electricity division, and Annex VII point 1. Decided here: (a) for ONE importer and ONE calendar year, whether the single mass-based de minimis exemption applies, on the caller's figures of net mass in whole kilograms of the importer's Annex I goods other than electricity and hydrogen, compared (not more than) with 1000 times the threshold in tonnes the caller states for the year; (b) for ONE import, whether its goods are outside the exemption (electricity, hydrogen), counted toward the threshold, or not goods of this Regulation. Which date puts an import into a year is not chosen: an unsure date basis that decides goes to a lawyer (needs_legal_review). Out of scope: the Commission's review of the threshold (Art 2a(3), Annex VII point 2), the exclusions of Art 2(2) to (12), CN-code classification under Annex I, who is the importer, authorised CBAM declarant status (Arts 4, 5, 17) and the annual CBAM declaration (Art 6).

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 188 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Every citation record quotes its statute word for word against the pinned text under `sources/` (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
