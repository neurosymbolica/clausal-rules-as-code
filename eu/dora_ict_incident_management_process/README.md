# eu/dora_ict_incident_management_process

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

For ONE financial entity presupposed to be within the scope of DORA, which duties of Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA) Article 17 (the ICT-related incident management process: paragraph 1, the two sentences of paragraph 2, and paragraph 3, points (a) to (f)) and Article 18(2) (the classification of cyber threats as significant) apply to it (all ten, always, the entities of Article 16(1) included), whether it has performed all of them, and which are outstanding, each duty being a tri-state judgement of the caller (true, false, unsure; unsure is referred to a lawyer). Out of scope: whether the entity is a financial entity within scope (Articles 2 and 3), the classification of one incident under Article 18(1) and its reporting (the order on incident reporting), the mandates of Article 18(3) and (4), the simplified framework of Article 16, proportionality under Article 4, Articles 10, 11, 13 and 14, the delegated regulations 2024/1772 and 2024/1774, and dates of application.

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.
