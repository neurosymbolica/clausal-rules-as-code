# eu/dora_rts_policies_and_risk_management

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

The duties that Articles 1 to 3 of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1774 (the RTS on ICT risk management tools, methods, processes and policies) place on ONE financial entity: taking its size, overall risk profile and complexity into account (Article 1, for Title II only), the general elements of its ICT security policies, procedures, protocols and tools (Article 2(1) and 2(2)) and its ICT risk management policies and procedures (Article 3, both paragraphs): twenty-seven duties, each a caller judgement (true, false or unsure), behind the gate of DORA Article 16(1), first subparagraph (an entity listed there applies the simplified framework of Title III instead, answered as simplified_framework_entity). Three queries: which duties are required, whether all are performed, and which are outstanding. An unsure judgement is handed to a lawyer (needs_legal_review, open_textured); no crisp test is invented. Out of scope: whether the entity is a financial entity within DORA (presupposed), the other articles of the RTS (Articles 4 to 42), the simplified framework and the qualification of the entities of DORA Article 16(1), the duties of DORA itself, and any date. ## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.
