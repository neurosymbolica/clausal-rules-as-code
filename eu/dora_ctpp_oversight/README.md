# eu/dora_ctpp_oversight

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

The oversight framework of Articles 32 to 35 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA) for ONE ICT third-party service provider designated as critical (THE PROVIDER): the duties those Articles place on THE PROVIDER (Art 35(1), point (d), closing words; Art 35(5)); the powers the Lead Overseer has in respect of it (Art 35(1), points (a) to (d) and (d)(iv)); whether its two windows to answer the Lead Overseer are open (Art 33(4), fourth sentence; Art 35(3), second sentence); when the ground for a periodic penalty payment is met (Art 35(6)); the outer period and the daily ceiling of such a payment (Art 35(7); Art 35(8), first sentence); and when competent authorities need the Lead Overseer's agreement for a measure concerning THE PROVIDER (Art 33(5)). The designation itself (Art 31) is the caller's statement in the key of the order eu/dora-ctpp-designation. Out of scope (see the deferred and excluded sets): the Oversight Forum, the Lead Overseer's assessment, plan adoption and procedure, the JON, the areas of recommendation of point (d)(i) to (iii), the amount within the ceiling and its criteria, enforcement, disclosure and the right to be heard, NIS2, Articles 36 to 44 and the financial ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 215 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Every citation record quotes its statute word for word against the pinned text under `sources/` (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
