merger_and_split_allocation EXPANSION

reason
Art 3(8)-(9) say how prior de minimis aid is attributed when undertakings merge, are acquired or split. This domain takes the grant list as already attributed to the undertaking being assessed.
consequence
After a merger, acquisition or split, the caller must build the grant list per Art 3(8)-(9). A list built otherwise gives a cumulation total for the wrong set of grants.

Defers

  • 32023R2831#parag_003.008

    8. In the case of mergers or acquisitions, all prior de minimis aid granted to any of the merging undertakings shall be taken into account when determining whether any new de minimis aid to the new or the acquiring undertaking exceeds the ceiling laid down in paragraph 2. De minimis aid lawfully granted before the merger or acquisition shall remain lawful.

  • 32023R2831#parag_003.009

    9. If one undertaking splits into two or more separate undertakings, de minimis aid granted before the split shall be allocated to the undertaking that benefited from it, which is in principle the undertaking taking over the activities for which the de minimis aid was used. If such an allocation is not possible, the de minimis aid shall be allocated proportionately on the basis of the book value of the equity capital of the new undertakings at the effective date of the split.

aid_amount_and_grant_date_inputs EXPANSION

reason
Art 3(3), 3(5) and 3(6) fix how an aid amount and its date are established: the moment the legal right is conferred, the gross grant equivalent, and the discounted value of instalments. Each grant arrives here as a date and an amount already established that way.
consequence
The caller must supply, for each grant, the date the legal right was conferred and the gross grant equivalent discounted to that date. Payment dates, net amounts or undiscounted instalments give a wrong total.

single_undertaking_grouping AUTHORIZED

reason
Which legal entities form one single undertaking is supplied by the caller as a pre-grouped grant list; it is not derived here.
consequence
If the caller passes the grants of only one entity of a group, the aid of the other linked entities is not counted, and the ceiling test can pass when it should fail.

sector_ceilings AUTHORIZED

reason
Only the general EUR 300 000 ceiling of Regulation 2023/2831 is applied.
consequence
Aid to an undertaking in a sector with its own de minimis regulation is tested against the general ceiling, which can be the wrong line for that sector.
