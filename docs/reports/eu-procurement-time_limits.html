subcentral_mutual_agreement_deadline AUTHORIZED

reason
Art 28(4) lets a sub-central contracting authority set the tender deadline by mutual agreement with the candidates invited, in place of the Art 28(2) minimum. The order excludes it because the profile carries neither the contracting-authority type nor the agreement input the branch needs.
consequence
A sub-central authority that HAS reached such an agreement is governed by the agreed deadline, not by the minimum this domain reports. The verdict here is the Directive's floor for the general case, and a caller in that position must not read it as binding.

conduct_of_negotiation_dialogue_and_partnership AUTHORIZED

reason
Arts 29(2)-(4) and (6), 30(2)-(8) and 31(2)-(3) and (5)-(7) govern the CONDUCT and STRUCTURE of the competitive procedure with negotiation, the competitive dialogue and the innovation partnership: who may tender, the duty to negotiate, staging, confidentiality, prizes to dialogue participants, partnership phasing and its proportionality. Not one of them prescribes a day count. This domain's entire input surface is (procedure, stage, three circumstance flags, observed days) and its outputs are a minimum day count, a status and a conformity, so none of these provisions is reachable by it (ruling 2026-09-08).
consequence
A conforming verdict from this domain says the day counts clear the Arts 27-31 floors. It says NOTHING about whether the negotiation or dialogue was lawfully conducted, staged or concluded; a caller must apply those provisions itself.

general_adequacy_and_extension_duties AUTHORIZED

reason
Art 47 imposes a standalone duty to set ADEQUATE time limits and to EXTEND them in named circumstances. That is a separate deontic determination from the minimum-days tables the order scopes here.
consequence
THE SHARP EDGE. Meeting the minimum in Arts 27-31 does NOT discharge Art 47: a limit can clear the floor and still be inadequate to the complexity of the procurement, or require extension because site visits were needed or documents changed late. A `minimum_days_prescribed` verdict is a floor, never a finding that the limit set was lawful.

calendar_deadline_computation AUTHORIZED

reason
This domain reports a minimum DAY COUNT. Turning that count plus a dispatch date into a calendar deadline is governed by the periods-and-dates Regulation -- which day the period starts, how weekends and public holidays fall, when it expires -- and the order scopes it out.
consequence
A caller must do the calendar arithmetic itself under Reg 1182/71. The commonest error this prevents is assuming the period starts on the dispatch date; it does not.

The information provided shall be sufficiently precise to enable economic operators to identify the nature and scope of the required solution and decide whether to request to participate in the procedure. The contracting authority may decide to set up the innovation partnership with one partner or with several partners conducting separate research and development activities. The minimum time limit for receipt of requests to participate shall be 30 days from the date on which the contract notice is sent. Only those economic operators invited by the contracting authority following the assessment of the information provided may participate in the procedure. Contracting authorities may limit the number of suitable candidates to be invited to participate in the procedure in accordance with Article 65. The contracts shall be awarded on the sole basis of the award criterion of the best price-quality ratio in accordance with Article 67.

2. The innovation partnership shall aim at the development of an innovative product, service or works and the subsequent purchase of the resulting supplies, services or works, provided that they correspond to the performance levels and maximum costs agreed between the contracting authorities and the participants. The innovation partnership shall be structured in successive phases following the sequence of steps in the research and innovation process, which may include the manufacturing of the products, the provision of the services or the completion of the works. The innovation partnership shall set intermediate targets to be attained by the partners and provide for payment of the remuneration in appropriate instalments. Based on those targets, the contracting authority may decide after each phase to terminate the innovation partnership or, in the case of an innovation partnership with several partners, to reduce the number of partners by terminating individual contracts, provided that the contracting authority has indicated in the procurement documents those possibilities and the conditions for their use.

4. During the negotiations, contracting authorities shall ensure the equal treatment of all tenderers. To that end, they shall not provide information in a discriminatory manner which may give some tenderers an advantage over others. They shall inform all tenderers whose tenders have not been eliminated, pursuant to paragraph 5, in writing of any changes to the technical specifications or other procurement documents other than those setting out the minimum requirements. Following those changes, contracting authorities shall provide sufficient time for tenderers to modify and re-submit amended tenders, as appropriate. In accordance with Article 21, contracting authorities shall not reveal to the other participants confidential information communicated by a candidate or tenderer participating in the negotiations without its agreement. Such agreement shall not take the form of a general waiver but shall be given with reference to the intended communication of specific information.
