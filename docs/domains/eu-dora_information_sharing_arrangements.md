## Scope

Article 45 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA), information-sharing arrangements on cyber threat information and intelligence. Decided here: (1) information_sharing_within_article_45 -- whether an exchange is one that Article 45(1), points (a) to (c), read with Article 45(2) as part of point (c), covers (all five conditions required; a false condition decides alone; an absent condition is named before an unsure one is referred); sharing_outside_article_45 means only not covered, never forbidden. (2) membership_notification_duty -- which notification Article 45(3) requires on validation of membership or once its cessation takes effect. Out of scope (handed to the caller or a lawyer): who is a financial entity (presupposed), what a cyber threat is, which authority is competent, the time for notifying, and whether rules of conduct respect Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and competition guidelines; every unsure judgement is referred as needs_legal_review, never decided.
