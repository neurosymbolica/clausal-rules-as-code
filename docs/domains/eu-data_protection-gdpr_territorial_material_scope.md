## Scope

Whether Regulation (EU) 2016/679 applies to a processing profile (Art 2(1) material scope, Art 2(2) and 2(3) exclusions, Art 3(1)-(3) territorial limbs), which ground made it apply or took it out, and whether the controller or processor must designate a representative in the Union under Art 27(1) and (2). Out of scope, handed to sibling orders or a lawyer: lawfulness of processing (Art 6), transfers (Chapter V), what a designated representative does, the Member State of the representative (Art 27(3)), and the instrument that governs processing taken out by Art 2(2)(d) or Art 2(3).
