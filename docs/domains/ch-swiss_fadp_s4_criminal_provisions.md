## Scope

Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (DSG, SR 235.1, German text, fedlex consolidation Stand 7 July 2025) Arts 60 to 66, criminal provisions, on given facts: whether conduct falls under the one examined offence of Arts 60(1)(a), 60(1)(b) numbers 1 and 2, 60(2), 61(a) to (c), 62(1), 62(2) or 63 (fine of up to 250 000 francs); whether its prosecution needs a criminal complaint and whether one was filed; whether the five-year limitation period of Art 66 has run between two dates; and whether under Art 64(2) the authority may order the business to pay the fine (fine in consideration at most 50 000 francs, inclusive). For comparison, the ceiling of administrative fines under EU GDPR Art 83(4), (5) and (6), and whether each law provides a fine on the same facts. Out of scope (handed to the lawyer): the amount of any fine; who in a business is punishable under VStrR Arts 6 and 7 (Art 64(1)); the competent authority (Art 65); the duties the offences refer to; Swiss Criminal Code rules on intent, complaint and limitation; whether an EU fine is imposed and its amount; GDPR Art 84 penalties under Member State law.

## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "persons_punishable_in_businesses"

    **Why:** DSG Art 64(1) makes Arts 6 and 7 of the Administrative Criminal Law Act apply to offences committed in businesses: who in a business is punishable (the person who acted and, under VStrR Art 6, others) and when the business itself is liable. The VStrR is not pinned, so the order does not formalise it (reading (7), open question Q8).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says which persons in a business are punishable.

??? info "eu_fine_effective_proportionate_dissuasive"

    **Why:** GDPR Art 83(1) requires each fine to be effective, proportionate and dissuasive in each individual case: a standard for the fine imposed, not for the ceiling this order answers.

    **Consequence for a user:** The EU answer is a ceiling, not a fine.

??? info "eu_whether_and_how_much_to_fine"

    **Why:** GDPR Art 83(2) says fines are imposed in addition to or instead of the Art 58(2) measures, depending on the circumstances, and lists the matters weighed in deciding whether to fine and how much (points (a) to (k)); the order answers the ceiling only. Point (b) is quoted for reading (9) (intent is weighed, not required).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says whether an EU fine is imposed or how much it is.

??? info "eu_several_infringements"

    **Why:** GDPR Art 83(3) caps the total fine for several infringements in the same or linked processing operations at the amount for the gravest; the order answers one infringement at a time.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer combines several EU infringements.
