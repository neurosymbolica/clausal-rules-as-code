## Scope

Article 31 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA), read with its Articles 2 and 3 for the categories of financial entities and the definitions the readings name, and Articles 1 to 3 and 5 of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1502 (THE CRITERIA REGULATION). For ONE undertaking providing ICT services (THE PROVIDER) it decides: whether Article 31(8) excludes THE PROVIDER from designation as a critical ICT third-party service provider and otherwise whether it fulfils every 'step 1' sub-criterion, so that the ESAs go on to the 'step 2' assessment (ctpp_designation_criteria_status/3); which 'step 1' sub-criteria the stated figures fulfil, and in which categories of financial entities (ctpp_step_1_indicators/3); which ESA Article 31(1), point (b), appoints as Lead Overseer, or that the largest sums are tied (ctpp_lead_overseer/3); and which duties Article 31(4), the last sentence of Article 31(5) and Article 31(13) place on a designated provider (ctpp_designated_provider_duties/3). ## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "article_31_5_notification_procedure"

    **Why:** Art 31(5), last sentence (THE PROVIDER notifies the financial entities to which it provides services of its designation as critical), is formalised (reading (15)). Not formalised here: the Lead Overseer's notification of the outcome of the assessment, THE PROVIDER's reasoned statement within 6 weeks, the Lead Overseer's request for information within 30 calendar days, the ESAs' notification of the designation and of the starting date of oversight, and that date no later than one month after the notification. They are steps of the authorities' procedure, with its periods.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer computes a period of the designation procedure or the start of oversight.

??? info "article_31_12_use_condition"

    **Why:** Art 31(12) is read only to identify the critical provider established in a third country to which Art 31(13) refers (reading (15)). Its rule, that financial entities shall only make use of the services of such a provider if it has established a subsidiary in the Union within the 12 months following the designation, binds the financial entities and is not formalised here.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says whether a financial entity may use the services of a critical provider established in a third country.

??? info "step_2_assessment"

    **Why:** Articles 2(5) (sub-criteria 1.3 and 1.4), 3(4) (sub-criterion 2.3), 4 (sub-criterion 3.1) and 5(5) (the 'step 2' sub-criterion of Article 31(2), point (d)(i)) of THE CRITERIA REGULATION: the 'step 2' assessment the ESAs carry out in the light of the intensity of the impact of discontinuing the services, the dependence on the same subcontractors, the interdependence of the systemic institutions, the critical nature of the services and the lack of real alternatives. They are the ESAs' assessment (reading (1)).

    **Consequence for a user:** step_1_sub_criteria_fulfilled is not a designation: it says that the ESAs go on to this assessment (open question (Q5)).

??? info "information_sources"

    **Why:** Article 6 of THE CRITERIA REGULATION: the ESAs use the data of the registers of information of Art 28(3) of the Regulation and other available data, and take the most recent data or the data made available by 31 December of the year preceding the assessment. These address the ESAs; the caller's figures stand for the data the ESAs use.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer checks where the figures come from or which year they describe.

??? info "entry_into_force"

    **Why:** Article 7 of THE CRITERIA REGULATION: entry into force on the twentieth day following publication, and the Lead Overseer applies sub-criterion 1.4 (a 'step 2' sub-criterion) as of 16 January 2025. The publication date is not in the pinned text; the order reads no date (reading (18)).

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers take THE CRITERIA REGULATION as in force (open question (Q16)).

??? info "article_2_1_entities_read_for_categories"

    **Why:** Art 2(1), points (a) to (u), and Art 2(2) of the Regulation are read only for the categories of financial entities to which THE CRITERIA REGULATION refers (reading (5)), for which types of THE PROVIDER are financial entities (reading (2)) and for the kinds of points (g) to (j) (reading (9)). Which entities the Regulation applies to is not formalised here.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says whether an entity is within the scope of the Regulation; THE PROVIDER's type is the caller's statement (open question (Q15)).

??? info "article_2_4_member_state_option"

    **Why:** Art 2(4) of the Regulation is read only for reading (5): the institutions exempted pursuant to Directive 2013/36/EU, which Member States may exclude from the scope of the Regulation, are named in no point of Article 2(1) and so are no category; the Member States' option and the information of the Commission are not formalised.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says whether a Member State has excluded an entity from the scope of the Regulation; institution_exempted_pursuant_to_directive_2013_36 is no value of this order (reading (5)).

??? info "definitions_read_through_the_caller"

    **Why:** Art 3, points (19) (ICT third-party service provider), (20) (ICT intra-group service provider) and (32) (institution exempted pursuant to Directive 2013/36/EU) are quoted for readings (2), (3) and (5). Whether THE PROVIDER meets the definitions of points (19) and (20) is the caller's statement (the type and the intra-group fact); point (32) only names the institutions that reading (5) finds in no point of Article 2(1).

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers take THE PROVIDER's type and the intra-group fact as given (open question (Q15)).

??? info "article_31_lead_words_and_criteria_points_without_own_key"

    **Why:** The lead words of Art 31(1) (the ESAs act through the Joint Committee upon recommendation from the Oversight Forum) and points (a) to (d) of Art 31(2), with their sub-points, have no citation key of their own in the order's citation vocabulary. Their content is applied through the keys eu_reg_2022_2554_art_31_1_a, eu_reg_2022_2554_art_31_1_b and eu_reg_2022_2554_art_31_2 and through Articles 2, 3 and 5 of THE CRITERIA REGULATION, which specify the criteria as 'step 1' sub-criteria (readings (1), (6) to (12)); criterion (c) has no 'step 1' sub-criterion of its own (Article 1(1), closing words). The institutional procedure of the lead words is the ESAs' act.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers apply the criteria only through the 'step 1' sub-criteria of THE CRITERIA REGULATION; no answer cites these units separately.
