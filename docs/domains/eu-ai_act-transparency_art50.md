## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "definitions_applied_by_the_caller"

    **Why:** The definitions of provider, placing on the market, emotion recognition system, biometric categorisation system, deep fake and general-purpose AI system are quoted because the keys use these terms; the caller applies them when it gives a key. The order does not formalise them.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers rest on the caller's application of these definitions in the keys it gives.

??? info "design_and_development_of_article_50_1"

    **Why:** Art 50(1) requires providers to ensure that the AI system is 'designed and developed in such a way' that the natural persons concerned are informed; the order reads that duty only through whether the required information is given (Q17). How the system is designed and developed to that end is not checked.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answer compliant for interaction_information says that the information was given as Art 50(5) requires; it does not say how the system was designed and developed.

??? info "technical_solutions_of_article_50_2"

    **Why:** The second sentence of Art 50(2) (providers shall ensure their technical solutions are effective, interoperable, robust and reliable as far as technically feasible, taking into account the specificities and limitations of various types of content, the costs of implementation and the generally acknowledged state of the art, as may be reflected in relevant technical standards) gives no crisp test; the first sentence (the marking duty) and the third (its exceptions) are formalised (Q17).

    **Consequence for a user:** The answer compliant for machine_readable_marking says only that the outputs are marked and detectable; it does not say that the technical solutions meet the second sentence.

??? info "data_protection_limb_of_article_50_3"

    **Why:** The first sentence of Art 50(3) also requires deployers to process the personal data in accordance with Regulations (EU) 2016/679 and (EU) 2018/1725 and Directive (EU) 2016/680, as applicable; that is judged under those acts, which are not pinned. The information duty and its exception are formalised.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answer compliant for emotion_recognition_or_biometric_categorisation_information does not say that the personal data were processed lawfully.
