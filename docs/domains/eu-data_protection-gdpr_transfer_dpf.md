## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "decision_monitoring_and_review"

    **Why:** Art 3 (its heading, and paragraphs 1 to 4: the Commission's monitoring, the exchange of information with the Member States and the periodic review) is operative text with legal consequences that no limb of this order formalises; the outcome of a review reaches the order only through the attested fact data_privacy_framework_decision_in_force (readings (4) and (8)).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer follows the Commission's monitoring or review; a caller states their outcome as data_privacy_framework_decision_in_force (Q4).

??? info "decision_suspension_amendment_repeal"

    **Why:** Art 3(5) (the Commission may suspend, amend or repeal the decision, or limit its scope, in accordance with Art 45(5) GDPR) is read for the meaning of the attested fact data_privacy_framework_decision_in_force but not formalised: the effect of a suspension, amendment, repeal or limitation arrives through that fact attested false (reading (4)).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer decides whether the Commission has suspended, amended, repealed or limited the decision: the caller states it as data_privacy_framework_decision_in_force (Q4).

??? info "article_45_machinery"

    **Why:** The Art 45 article heading, Art 45(2) (the elements of the Commission's assessment), (4) to (7) (monitoring, repeal, amendment or suspension and consultations) and (9) (older decisions) are read by no limb of this order; the Commission's acts under them reach this order only through data_privacy_framework_decision_in_force (reading (4)).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer checks the Commission's assessment or its review of the decision.

??? info "decision_recitals"

    **Why:** The recitals (1) to (223) set out the Commission's findings on the Framework and on U.S. law; they are not operative and no limb applies them. Recitals (7) to (10) and (52) to (55) are quoted for readings (2), (3) and (5) and reach the order only through the meanings of recipient_on_data_privacy_framework_list and certification_covers_transferred_data.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer applies a recital as a rule; their content reaches the order only through the attested outside facts.

??? info "decision_footnotes"

    **Why:** The footnotes of the decision give sources, references and explanations (footnote (14): the EEA States; footnote (15): the exporter's own duties under Regulation (EU) 2016/679); none is applied by a limb. Footnote (14) is quoted for open question (Q1).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer reads a footnote; a caller who treats a transfer from an EEA State as one from the Union states transfer_from_union true (Q1).

??? info "decision_annex_i_principles"

    **Why:** Annex I (the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework Principles and Supplemental Principles, their footnotes and the arbitral model) sets the duties of a certified organisation; no limb of this order applies it. Section I, points 3 (the List) and 6 (the human-resources limit) are quoted for readings (3) and (5) and reach the order only through recipient_on_data_privacy_framework_list and certification_covers_transferred_data.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer assesses an organisation's compliance with the Principles; what the List and a certification cover is stated by the caller as an attested outside fact.

??? info "decision_annexes_ii_to_viii"

    **Why:** Annexes II to VIII (the U.S. government's letters and representations: the Department of Commerce, the Federal Trade Commission, the Department of Transportation, the Department of Justice and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence on access by U.S. public authorities, and the list of abbreviations) describe the U.S. framework on which the Commission based its finding; no limb of this order applies them.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer assesses U.S. law; the Commission's finding in Article 1 is taken as the decision states it, while data_privacy_framework_decision_in_force says whether it is in force.
