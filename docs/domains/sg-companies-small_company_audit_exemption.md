## Scope

Whether a Singapore company is exempt from audit under the Companies Act 1967 (ss 205B and 205C and the Thirteenth Schedule): whether it is a small company or a small group, judged on the figures of its financial years. For ONE company and its assessed financial year. Drafter's reading, not a lawyer's.
