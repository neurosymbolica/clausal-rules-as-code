## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2022/1925 (Digital Markets Act), Art 5(1), Art 5(2) with its points (a) to (d), and Art 2(32). Three questions for one gatekeeper, one core platform service, one practice and one group of end users: whether the Article 5 obligations apply to the service (a Commission designation decision listing it, an attested outside fact); whether Art 5(2) prohibits the practice, allows it because the end user was presented with the specific choice and gave consent, allows it because the gatekeeper may rely on Article 6(1), point (c), (d) or (e), of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, or does not reach it; and whether, after a refusal or withdrawal of consent, the gatekeeper may repeat its request for the same purpose on a given day within the one-year period, counted from the refusal to its anniversary. Out of scope: Art 5(3) to (10); the other definitions of Art 2 (premises of the caller); Article 3 designation and its compliance period; whether consent or an Article 6(1) basis exists under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (attested outside facts); Regulation (EEC, Euratom) No 1182/71 on periods. An unsure practice or an unsure specific choice is handed to a lawyer.

## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "other_obligations_of_article_5"

    **Why:** Art 5(3) to (10) set the other obligations of Article 5 (prices and conditions offered elsewhere, communicating with end users, access through business users' applications, raising issues with authorities, identification, browser engine and payment services, tying of further core platform services, and information for advertisers and publishers). They are formalisable but are not part of this order's questions.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers never say whether the gatekeeper complies with Art 5(3) to (10); practice_outside_article_5_2 says only that Art 5(2) does not reach the practice.

??? info "definitions_not_read"

    **Why:** Article 2 defines, among others, gatekeeper (point (1)), core platform service (point (2)), end user (point (20)), business user (point (21)), personal data (point (25)) and profiling (point (31)). This order reads only point (32), consent; that the undertaking is a gatekeeper and the service a core platform service, and what counts as an end user and as personal data, are the caller's premises (reading (2)), and the designation is an outside fact (reading (3)).

    **Consequence for a user:** The caller establishes the premises first; the answers rest on them without checking them.
