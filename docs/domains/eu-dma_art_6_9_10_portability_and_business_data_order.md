## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2022/1925 (Digital Markets Act), Art 6(1), 6(9) and 6(10), with Art 2(25) and 2(32). For one profile (one gatekeeper, one core platform service, one request by one requester, one set of requested data) the order decides three status questions with a cited answer: whether the obligations of Article 6 apply to the service (it is listed in a Commission designation decision, an attested outside fact); whether the gatekeeper meets the duty of Art 6(9) to provide effective portability of end user data, free of charge, with tools and continuous and real-time access, on request; and whether it meets the duty of Art 6(10) to provide a business user or a third party it authorised with free, effective, high-quality, continuous and real-time access to and use of data, with the personal-data limits of the second subparagraph (direct connection and end user opt-in consent, the consent an attested outside fact). Open-textured judgements are the caller's (true, false or unsure) and an unsure judgement that decides is referred to a lawyer. ## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "article_6_heading"

    **Why:** The heading of Article 6 names its obligations as obligations for gatekeepers 'susceptible of being further specified under Article 8'. It sets no obligation itself, and Article 8 is not pinned here (see the excluded set).

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers never take account of any measure specified under Article 8 (Q9).

??? info "other_obligations_of_article_6"

    **Why:** Art 6(2) to (8) and (11) to (13) set the other obligations of Article 6 (no use of business users' non-public data in competition with them, un-installing applications and changing default settings, third-party applications and application stores, ranking, switching between applications and services, interoperability, access for advertisers and publishers to performance measuring tools and data, access for online search engines to ranking, query, click and view data, fair general conditions of access, and conditions of termination). They are formalisable but are not part of this order's questions.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers never say whether the gatekeeper complies with Art 6(2) to (8) or (11) to (13).

??? info "definitions_not_read"

    **Why:** Article 2 defines, among others, gatekeeper (point (1)), core platform service (point (2)), end user (point (20)), business user (point (21)), data (point (24)) and non-personal data (point (26)). This order reads only point (25), personal data, and point (32), consent; that the undertaking is a gatekeeper and the service a core platform service, and who counts as an end user and as a business user, are the caller's premises (reading (2)), and the designation is an outside fact (reading (3)).

    **Consequence for a user:** The caller establishes the premises first; the answers rest on them without checking them.
