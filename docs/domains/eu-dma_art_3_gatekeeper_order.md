## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2022/1925 (Digital Markets Act) Art 3(1), 3(2), 3(5) and 3(9), for ONE undertaking and ONE core platform service it provides: whether the impact threshold of Art 3(2)(a), the gateway thresholds of Art 3(2)(b) and the entrenchment condition of Art 3(2)(c) are met; whether the undertaking is presumed to satisfy the requirements of Art 3(1) (all three thresholds met); whether the requirements of Art 3(1) are met, by the presumption with the rebuttal of Art 3(5) (the Commission's conclusion as an attested outside fact) or by the three open-textured judgements of Art 3(1) handed to the user (tri-state, unsure referred to a lawyer); and whether a Commission designation decision under Art 3(9) designates the undertaking for that service (an attested outside fact, never decided here). Out of scope: the notification duty and time limits of Art 3(3), (4) and (10), the delegated acts of Art 3(6) and (7), designation without the thresholds under Art 3(8), the definitions of Art 2 and the user-counting methodology of the Annex (the caller's premises).

## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "notification_to_the_commission"

    **Why:** Art 3(3) requires an undertaking that meets all the thresholds to notify the Commission within 2 months, and sets out what follows a failure to notify. The duty and its time limit are formalisable but are not part of this order's questions; the order uses only the word 'notification' of Art 3(5) (reading (7)).

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers never say whether or when a notification is due or was made.

??? info "designation_without_the_thresholds"

    **Why:** Art 3(8) lets the Commission designate, after the procedure of Article 17, an undertaking that meets the requirements of Art 3(1) but not each threshold, weighing the elements listed in points (a) to (g). It is the Commission's assessment; the order says only whether the three judgements of Art 3(1) are all true (reading (7), Q8).

    **Consequence for a user:** requirements_met without the presumption is not a designation; gatekeeper_status reads the designation as an outside fact. The elements (a) to (g) are not read: the three judgement keys of Art 3(1) are the caller's reading of the undertaking, which may rest on them.

??? info "compliance_period_after_listing"

    **Why:** Art 3(10) requires the gatekeeper to comply with Articles 5, 6 and 7 within 6 months after a core platform service is listed in the designation decision. The time limit is formalisable but is not part of this order's questions.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers never say from when the obligations of Articles 5 to 7 apply.
