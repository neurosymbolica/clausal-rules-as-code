## Scope

For ONE ICT-related incident of ONE financial entity: whether each of the eight conditions that Article 8(1) of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1772 reads to decide a major incident is met -- the critical services affected (Article 6) and the materiality thresholds of Article 9(1) to (6), measured as Articles 1 to 7 say, under Article 18(1) of Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA) (dora_incident_threshold_status/4); and whether the final report on a major ICT-related incident was submitted within the one-month time limit of Article 5(1)(c) of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/301, with the weekend and bank holiday rule of Article 5(4) to (6) (dora_final_report_timeliness/5). It does NOT decide whether the incident is major (Art 8(1) itself, formalised in the order on DORA incident reporting), recurring incidents (Art 8(2)), the classification of cyber threats (DORA Art 18(2)), the time limits of the initial notification and intermediate report and the notice of a delay (Art 5(1)(a), (b), (2), (3) of 2025/301), the reporting templates of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/302, or whether the entity is a financial entity within the scope of DORA. ## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "cyber_threats_and_standards_mandate"

    **Why:** DORA Art 18(2) is the classification of cyber threats as significant; Art 18(3) and (4) give the European Supervisory Authorities the mandate to draft the technical standards and the Commission the power to adopt them.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer classifies a cyber threat.

??? info "recurring_incidents"

    **Why:** Art 8(2) of Delegated Regulation 2024/1772 counts recurring incidents as one major incident where they meet all of its conditions; its point (c) is the major-incident test of Art 8(1), which this order does not apply (reading (14)).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says whether recurring incidents count as one major incident.

??? info "initial_and_intermediate_report_limits"

    **Why:** Art 5(1)(a) and (b) and (2) of Delegated Regulation 2025/301 are the time limits of the initial notification and the intermediate report, formalised in the order on DORA incident reporting; Art 5(3) is the duty to inform the competent authority of a delay.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says whether the initial notification or the intermediate report was in time, or whether a delay was notified.

??? info "time_limits_lead_words"

    **Why:** The lead words of Art 5(1) of Delegated Regulation 2025/301 are read for the final report only (reading (12)); for the initial notification and the intermediate report they are read in the order on DORA incident reporting.

    **Consequence for a user:** None for the final report.
