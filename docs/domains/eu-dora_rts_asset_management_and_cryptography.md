## Scope

The duties of ONE financial entity under Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1774 (RTS on the ICT risk management framework) Articles 4 and 5 (ICT asset management policy and procedure) and Articles 6 and 7 (encryption, cryptographic controls and cryptographic key management), gated by Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA) Article 16(1), first subparagraph: an entity listed there answers simplified_framework_entity. Three queries: which duties apply (required), whether all are performed (complete / incomplete), and which are outstanding. Every duty is a caller judgement (true, false, unsure); an unsure judgement that decides is referred as needs_legal_review. The microenterprise exception of Art 4(2)(c) is the only condition. Out of scope: whether the entity is a financial entity within DORA (Articles 2 and 3), the simplified framework of DORA Art 16(1) second subparagraph, (2) and (3) and RTS Title III, the other RTS articles, and the DORA articles the text refers to (Articles 8, 9 and 10).

## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "simplified_framework_duties"

    **Why:** DORA Art 16(1), first subparagraph (Articles 5 to 15 of DORA do not apply to the entities it lists), is formalised as the gate of every query (reading (2)), standing for the rule that Title II of the RTS applies to financial entities other than those entities. Not formalised: who those entities are, which the caller states, and the lead words of the second subparagraph, which introduce the duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework; those are the subject of the order on proportionality and the simplified framework.

    **Consequence for a user:** simplified_framework_entity says only that RTS Articles 4 to 7 do not apply; it gives none of the duties the entity has instead.

??? info "simplified_framework_duty_list"

    **Why:** DORA Art 16(1), second subparagraph, points (a) to (h), list the duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework, and Art 16(2) requires that framework to be documented and reviewed. They bind only the entities of Article 16(1), for which every query answers simplified_framework_entity.

    **Consequence for a user:** The order does not check the simplified framework.
