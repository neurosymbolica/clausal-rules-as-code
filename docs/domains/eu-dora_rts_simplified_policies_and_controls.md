## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "simplified_framework_entities"

    **Why:** Art 16(1), first subparagraph (Articles 5 to 15 do not apply to the entities it lists), is formalised as the gate of every query (reading (1)). Not formalised: who those entities are, which the caller states, and the lead words of the second subparagraph, which introduce the duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework of DORA itself; those are the subject of the DORA order on proportionality and the simplified framework.

    **Consequence for a user:** not_article_16_1_entity says only that Title III of the RTS does not bind the entity; it gives none of the duties of Title II. PARTLY FORMALISED: the first subparagraph as the gate only.

??? info "simplified_framework_duty_list"

    **Why:** Art 16(1), second subparagraph, points (a) to (h), list the duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework in DORA, and Art 16(2) requires that framework to be documented and reviewed (reading (11)).

    **Consequence for a user:** complete and none_outstanding say nothing about these duties (open question (Q11)).

??? info "outsourced_verification_of_compliance"

    **Why:** Art 28(3) permits the outsourcing of the tasks of verifying compliance with ICT risk management requirements and keeps the entity fully responsible for that verification. It sets no duty with an output of its own (reading (10)).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer turns on it; a verification done by a provider is judged under Art 28(5) and (6) as done by the entity (open question (Q10)).
