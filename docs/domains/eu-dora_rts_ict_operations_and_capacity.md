## Scope

For ONE financial entity presupposed to be within the scope of DORA, which of the duties on ICT operations (Article 8(1) and 8(2), with its second subparagraph) and on capacity and performance management (Article 9(1) and 9(2)) of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1774 apply to it, whether it has performed all of them, and which are outstanding, each duty judged by the caller (true, false or unsure; unsure is referred to a lawyer as open_textured). The rule of DORA Article 16(1), first subparagraph, is the gate of every query: an entity it lists answers simplified_framework_entity. Out of scope: who those entities are and the simplified framework (DORA Art 16(1) second subparagraph, 16(2), 16(3), RTS Title III), the other articles of the RTS, DORA Articles 2 to 15, proportionality as a relief, and dates of application.

## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "simplified_framework_duties"

    **Why:** DORA Art 16(1), first subparagraph (Articles 5 to 15 of DORA do not apply to the entities it lists), is formalised as the gate of every query (reading (2)). Not formalised: who those entities are, which the caller states, and the lead words of the second subparagraph, which introduce the duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework; those are the subject of the order on proportionality and the simplified framework and of the order on Title III of the RTS.

    **Consequence for a user:** simplified_framework_entity says only that Articles 8 and 9 of the RTS do not apply; it gives none of the duties the entity has instead.

??? info "simplified_framework_duty_list"

    **Why:** DORA Art 16(1), second subparagraph, points (a) to (h), list the duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework, and Art 16(2) requires that framework to be documented and reviewed. They bind only the entities of Article 16(1), for which every query answers simplified_framework_entity.

    **Consequence for a user:** The order does not check the simplified framework.
