# The domains

Each domain answers one legal question. Its page shows the scope, the pinned sources, the interpretive choices, the provisions deferred and the result of every public check.

| Domain | Question |
|---|---|
| [`eu/aml/amlr_beneficial_ownership_threshold`](eu-aml-amlr_beneficial_ownership_threshold.md) | Does a person's effective holding reach the 25 % threshold (AMLR Art 52)? |
| [`eu/aml/amlr_bo_chain`](eu-aml-amlr_bo_chain.md) | Is a person a beneficial owner through direct and indirect holdings in a chain of entities (AMLR Art 52(1))? |
| [`eu/procurement/framework_agreements`](eu-procurement-framework_agreements.md) | Is a framework agreement's term, and a call-off under it, lawful (Directive 2014/24 Art 33)? |
| [`eu/procurement/life_cycle_costing`](eu-procurement-life_cycle_costing.md) | Is a life-cycle costing method valid, and what does it compute (Art 68)? |
| [`eu/procurement/shortlisting`](eu-procurement-shortlisting.md) | May the authority limit the number of candidates, and to how many (Art 65)? |
| [`eu/procurement/time_limits`](eu-procurement-time_limits.md) | What is the minimum time limit for receipt (Art 27-31)? |
| [`eu/procurement/utilities_negotiated_without_prior_call`](eu-procurement-utilities_negotiated_without_prior_call.md) | May a utility negotiate without a prior call for competition (Directive 2014/25 Art 50)? |
| [`eu/state_aid/de_minimis_cumulation`](eu-state_aid-de_minimis_cumulation.md) | How much de minimis aid has an undertaking received in the rolling three-year window, and is it under the ceiling (Regulation (EU) 2023/2831)? |

`eu/procurement/directive_2014_24/` is the shared vocabulary the procurement domains import.
