## Scope

Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS 2) Art 20(1)-(2) and Art 21(1)-(4): a skeleton of the duties of the management bodies and of the cybersecurity risk-management measures of an essential or important entity. It lists the duties, the minimum measure areas (Art 21(2)(a)-(j)), the proportionality factors and the supply chain considerations, and states whether a corrective-measures duty arises on a finding of non-compliance. It never decides whether a measure is appropriate or proportionate, nor whether an entity complies; Art 21(2)(j) 'where appropriate' is handed to the user as a judgement key. Out of scope: entity class (sibling order nis2-scope-entity-class), Art 22(1) coordinated assessments, and the implementing acts under Art 21(5).

## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "implementing_acts_on_the_measures"

    **Why:** Art 21(5) requires the Commission to adopt implementing acts laying down the technical and methodological requirements of the Art 21(2) measures for DNS service providers, TLD name registries, cloud computing, data centre, content delivery network, managed service and managed security service providers, online marketplaces, online search engines, social networking services platforms and trust service providers, by 17 October 2024. Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2690 is such an act; it is pinned by automated retrieval but is NOT part of this order's extract, so its requirements are not applied.

    **Consequence for a user:** For those provider types the measures of Art 21(2) are further specified by Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2690, which the answers do not apply; the caller applies it itself.
