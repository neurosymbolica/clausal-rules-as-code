## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "content_of_sandbox_documentation_and_summary"

    **Why:** Art 59(1) is listed whole because the order keys the paragraph and four of its points only; points (a) to (g) are formalised through the keys of the query. Points (h) to (j) are read as done or not; the order does not check the content of the logs, the technical documentation of Annex IV or the published summary, and reads the exception of point (j) for sensitive operational data into its key.

    **Consequence for a user:** An answer sandbox_processing_permitted does not say that the documentation or the summary is complete; the caller checks it.

??? info "implementing_acts_and_other_law_on_testing"

    **Why:** Art 60(1) second to fourth sentences (Commission implementing acts on the plan; Union or national law on testing of Annex I products) and the words 'without prejudice to the prohibitions under Article 5' add no condition the order checks; the first sentence's route is read.

    **Consequence for a user:** An answer real_world_testing_permitted does not say that the practice is not prohibited by Art 5 or that the plan follows the implementing acts.

??? info "content_of_plan_registration_and_agreement"

    **Why:** Art 60(4) points (a), (c) and (h) are read as done or not; the content of the plan, of the registration (Annex IX, Art 49) and of the agreement with deployers is not checked.

    **Consequence for a user:** An answer real_world_testing_permitted does not say that the plan, the registration or the agreement is complete.

??? info "content_of_the_member_state_framework"

    **Why:** Art 60a(5) lists what a real-world testing framework must lay down; the order answers subject_to_the_member_state_framework without checking the framework or the testing against it (Q4).

    **Consequence for a user:** That answer does not say whether the testing meets the framework or the provisions of Art 60 it names.

??? info "entry_into_force_and_other_dates"

    **Why:** Art 113 first paragraph (entry into force) and third paragraph points (b) and (d) date provisions not in this order; point (c) is quoted for the high-risk dates of Q10 but no limb reads it; the general date of the first paragraph and point (a) are read.

    **Consequence for a user:** None for these answers.
