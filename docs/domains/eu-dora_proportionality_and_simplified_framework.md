## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (Digital Operational Resilience Act, DORA), pinned original OJ text 32022R2554 (OJ L 333, 27.12.2022, p. 1). For ONE entity of ONE entity type this order decides: whether the entity is one of those referred to in Article 16(1), first subparagraph, to which Articles 5 to 15 do not apply (simplified_framework_qualification/3, reading Art 2(1)(u), Art 2(2), Art 2(3)(c), Art 2(4), first sentence, and Article 3, points (32), (34), (36), (39) and (53)); which duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework of Art 16(1), second subparagraph, points (a) to (h) (point (h) as two duties), and Art 16(2) apply to it (simplified_framework_duties_required/3); whether it has performed all of them (simplified_framework_duties_complete/3, the compliance check); and which of them are outstanding (simplified_framework_duties_outstanding/3). The principle of proportionality of Art 4(1) is read inside the caller's judgement of each duty and removes no duty. ## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "member_state_notice_and_publication"

    **Why:** Art 2(4) is PARTLY FORMALISED. Its first sentence (a Member State may exclude the institutions of Article 2(5), points (4) to (23), of Directive 2013/36/EU) is read through the key exempted_from_dora_scope_by_member_state (reading (6)). Not formalised: its second and third sentences, by which the Member State informs the Commission and the Commission publishes the information.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer depends on whether the Commission was informed or the information published.

??? info "scope_rules_formalised_in_the_scope_order"

    **Why:** The points of Art 2(1) other than point (u), the lead words of Art 2(3) and its points other than point (c) are formalised in the DORA order on scope and entity types. This order reads Art 2(1), points (a) to (t), only as the source of the entity type atoms of that order, and presupposes that the exclusions of Art 2(3), points (a), (b), (d), (e) and (f), do not apply (reading (1)).

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers presuppose an entity in scope and not excluded by those points (open question (Q1)).

??? info "proportionality_other_chapters_and_authorities"

    **Why:** Art 4(2) applies the principle of proportionality to Chapters III, IV and V, Section I, which this order does not formalise; Art 4(3) binds the competent authorities when they review the framework on the reports of Article 6(5) and Article 16(2) (reading (15)).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer of this order depends on them (open question (Q14)).
