## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA), Articles 50 to 52, for one breach of the Regulation by a financial entity, from the pinned original OJ text 32022R2554. Given the national choices and facts the caller states, the order answers five questions: whether administrative penalty rules are required for the breach (Art 50(3) with the option of Art 52(1)); the minimum supervisory and investigatory powers (Art 50(2)) and administrative penalties and remedial measures (Art 50(4)) the competent authorities must have, with the extension of Art 50(5); the safeguards of Art 50(6) for a decision imposing measures of Art 50(2) point (c); the circumstances Art 51(2) requires the authority to take into account; and the liaison duties of Art 52(2). Out of scope: the general clause of Art 50(1), the quality of the penalties (Art 50(3) second sentence), the ways of exercise (Art 51(1)), the conditions national law sets, and Articles 35(6), 53 and 54; an unsure Art 51(2) appraisal is handed to a lawyer. Every reading is the drafter's, not a lawyer's.

## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "general_powers_clause"

    **Why:** Art 50(1) gives the competent authorities all supervisory, investigatory and sanctioning powers necessary to fulfil their duties under the Regulation. It lists no power of its own; reading (3) relies on it for the place of Art 50(2), point (c).

    **Consequence for a user:** minimum_powers gives the least powers the Regulation lists, not every power an authority needs.

??? info "quality_of_penalties"

    **Why:** PARTLY FORMALISED. Art 50(3), first sentence, the duty to lay down rules for administrative penalties and remedial measures without prejudice to Article 52, is formalised (reading (2)). Not formalised: the duty to ensure their effective implementation and the second sentence, that they be effective, proportionate and dissuasive.

    **Consequence for a user:** required says that rules must be laid down; it says nothing of their quality.

??? info "data_traffic_records_suspicion"

    **Why:** PARTLY FORMALISED. Art 50(4)(d) is listed where national law permits it (reading (4)). Not formalised: 'where there is a reasonable suspicion of a breach of this Regulation and where such records may be relevant to an investigation', read as a condition on using the power in a case.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answer says the power must exist; it does not say when it may be used.

??? info "extension_national_conditions"

    **Why:** PARTLY FORMALISED. Art 50(5), the extension to members of the management body and other responsible individuals, is formalised (reading (5)). Not formalised: 'subject to the conditions provided for in national law', and who under national law is responsible for the breach.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answer says the power must exist; the conditions and the persons are for national law.
