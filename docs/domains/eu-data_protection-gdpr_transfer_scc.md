## Scope

Whether one transfer of personal data from a data exporter to a data importer relies validly on the standard contractual clauses of Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914, so that Article 46(2)(c) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 provides appropriate safeguards: (A) which module of the clauses fits the roles of the Parties (Annex I module headings); (B) whether the clauses are available for the transfer under Article 1(1) of the decision; (C) whether the clauses, as used, provide the safeguards (Article 1(1), Clause 1(b), Clause 2(a), Clause 14 (a), (b) and (d) with the Module Four heading words); (D) whether the Appendix (Clause 1(d), Annexes I to III, Clause 9(a) options) is completed as the module requires. Out of scope: whether a transfer takes place and Chapter V as a whole (gdpr-chapter-v-transfers), the material and territorial scope of the GDPR (gdpr-territorial-material-scope, arriving as attested outside facts), the roles of controller and processor (stated by the caller), the assessment of the third country's laws and practices (an attested outside fact), the Parties' performance of the clauses, and Article 4 transitional rules.
