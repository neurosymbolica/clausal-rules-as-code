## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "art_7_3_right_to_withdraw_and_its_effect"

    **Why:** The first two sentences of Art 7(3) (the data subject has the right to withdraw consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before the withdrawal) state a right and an effect on EARLIER processing. The text gives no outcome for processing after the withdrawal, and a date comparison needs a ruling on the day of the withdrawal (open question Q4). The third and fourth sentences ARE formalised (the keys informed_of_withdrawal_right_before_consent and withdrawal_as_easy_as_giving_consent).

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers do not say whether processing on a given date was lawful given a withdrawal; the caller checks Art 7(3) first two sentences.
