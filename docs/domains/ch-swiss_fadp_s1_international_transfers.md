## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "amendment_of_annex_and_disclosures_already_made"

    **Why:** DSV Art 8(6) has Annex 1 amended where adequate protection is no longer ensured and says this has no effect on disclosures already made; the order has no date argument and the caller states the finding for the time of the disclosure (reading (3), open question Q10).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer compares the time of a disclosure with the time of an amendment of Annex 1.

??? info "annex_notes_on_directive_2016_680"

    **Why:** The notes *, ** and *** of DSV Annex 1 say whether the finding for an entry includes disclosures under, or in the cooperation provided for by, Directive (EU) 2016/680; the caller takes them into account in the outside fact federal_council_adequacy_listing_covers_disclosure (reading (3), open question Q11).

    **Consequence for a user:** The order does not itself test whether a disclosure falls under Directive (EU) 2016/680.

??? info "minimum_content_of_clauses_guarantees_and_rules"

    **Why:** DSV Art 9(1) lists the points that contract clauses and specific guarantees must at least contain; DSV Art 11(1) and (2) say binding corporate rules apply to all undertakings of the group and must contain those points and further information. The order does not check content: whether the instrument ensures suitable protection is the caller's judgement (reading (5)).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says whether clauses, guarantees or rules contain what they must.

??? info "measures_to_make_the_recipient_comply"

    **Why:** DSV Art 9(2) and Art 10(1) require the controller (and the processor, for contract clauses or standard clauses) to take appropriate measures to ensure that the recipient complies with the clauses or guarantees: a duty of the discloser that this order does not answer.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says whether the discloser took those measures.

??? info "notification_deemed_given"

    **Why:** DSV Art 9(3) deems the duty to inform the Commissioner fulfilled for further disclosures under the same clauses or guarantees, or within the same legal person or group; the order does not test it: the caller gives the crisp notification key true where it applies (reading (5), open question Q6).

    **Consequence for a user:** The order does not itself decide whether a notification counts as given.

??? info "eu_forms_of_appropriate_safeguards"

    **Why:** GDPR Art 46(2) lists the safeguards that need no specific authorisation, Art 46(3) those that need the authorisation of the competent supervisory authority, and Art 46(5) keeps earlier authorisations and decisions in force until amended, replaced or repealed; the order does not read them: whether appropriate safeguards are provided is the caller's judgement appropriate_safeguards_provided (reading (9), open question Q8).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says which form of safeguard was used or whether an authorisation was needed.
