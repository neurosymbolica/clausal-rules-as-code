## Scope

For ONE financial entity presupposed to be within the scope of DORA, the duties of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1774 Article 22 (the ICT-related incident management policy: the policy itself, points (a) to (e) and the secure retention of evidence) and Article 23 (roles and responsibilities, the detection mechanism of paragraph 2 points (a) to (d) and its automated alerts on data source and log integrity, protection of recordings, logging of each detected anomalous activity, the criteria to trigger detection and response, and the criticality of the services affected): which of the seventeen duties apply, whether all are performed, and which are outstanding, each performance being a caller judgement (true, false or unsure; an unsure judgement is referred to a lawyer as open_textured). ## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "simplified_framework_duties"

    **Why:** DORA Art 16(1), first subparagraph (Articles 5 to 15 do not apply to the entities it lists), is formalised as the gate of every query (reading (2)). Not formalised: who those entities are, which the caller states, and the lead words of the second subparagraph, which introduce the duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework; those are the subject of the order on proportionality and the simplified framework.

    **Consequence for a user:** simplified_framework_entity says only that RTS Articles 22 and 23 do not apply; it gives none of the duties the entity has instead.

??? info "simplified_framework_duty_list"

    **Why:** DORA Art 16(1), second subparagraph, points (a) to (h), list the duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework, among them that sources of ICT risk and anomalies are allowed to be promptly identified and detected and ICT-related incidents promptly handled (point (d)), and DORA Art 16(2) requires that framework to be documented and reviewed. They bind only the entities of DORA Article 16(1), for which every query answers simplified_framework_entity.

    **Consequence for a user:** The order does not check the simplified framework.
