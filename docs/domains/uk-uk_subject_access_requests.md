## Scope

UK law in force (drafter's reading, not a lawyer's). Two rules of the UK GDPR for requests by data subjects: (a) the search limit of Art 15(1A) (inserted by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 s.78), read per item: an item not described in Art 15(1) is not within the right; a described item that a reasonable and proportionate search would not let the controller provide is not due; otherwise it is due, the reasonable-and-proportionate judgement being the caller's (unsure goes to a lawyer); (b) the time for responding, Art 12(3) and (4) with the applicable time period of Art 12A (one month from the relevant time, extendable to three months by a timely notice with reasons where necessary for complexity or number; clock stopped while further information reasonably required is awaited), applying to requests received on or after 5 February 2026 (S.I. 2026/82 reg 2(h), reg 4(a)); earlier requests are reported as saved and no date is computed. For comparison, the order's own EU limb counts EU GDPR Art 12(3) by Regulation 1182/71 Art 3 for a plain request. ## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "uk_article_12_other_paragraphs"

    **Why:** UK Art 12(1), (2), (6A), (6B) and (7) (form of information, facilitating rights, icons) are not asked by this order.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says whether information was given in the right form.

??? info "uk_fee_and_identity_steps"

    **Why:** UK Art 12(5) (free of charge; a fee or a refusal for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests) and Art 12(6) (identity information; delay until identity is confirmed): whether the controller may charge a fee, refuse, or ask for identity information is not decided. uk_relevant_time/5 takes what happened as its IDENTITY and FEE arguments (reading (5)). Quoted for context with the deferred marker.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says whether a fee or an identity request was allowed.

??? info "uk_article_15_transfer_safeguards_and_copy"

    **Why:** UK Art 15(2) (safeguards for transfers), Art 15(3) (the copy, limited by s.78(1)(b) to personal data to which the data subject is entitled under Art 15(1)) and Art 15(4) (rights of others) are not asked by this order.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says whether a copy is due or what it may cost.

??? info "dpa_2018_part_3_and_part_4_amendments"

    **Why:** The time limits and the search limit for law enforcement processing (Data Protection Act 2018 Part 3, ss.45, 54) and intelligence services processing (Part 4, s.94), and the savings for those requests: a different regime from the UK GDPR, left out by the order's choice (2026-11-15). The four site-made unit ids (d27e...) are quoted text inserted into ss.54 and 94.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer covers a request under Part 3 or Part 4 of the Data Protection Act 2018.

??? info "duaa_section_77_articles_13_and_14"

    **Why:** s.77 amends UK GDPR Arts 13 and 14 (information to be provided; disproportionate effort), not the response time; reg 2(i) brings it into force. Left out by the order's choice (2026-11-15).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says what information must be provided under Arts 13 and 14.

??? info "eu_article_12_other_paragraphs"

    **Why:** EU Art 12 is pinned only for the EU limb of the comparison, which reads Art 12(3); the other paragraphs are not compared.

    **Consequence for a user:** compare_uk_eu_response_deadline/6 compares the response period only.
