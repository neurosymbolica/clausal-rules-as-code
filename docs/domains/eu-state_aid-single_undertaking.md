## Scope

Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/2831 (de minimis aid), Art 2(2): the definition of a single undertaking, its lead-in, points (a) to (d) and its closing sentence. Three questions are decided: whether one enterprise has a relationship of points (a) to (d) over another (direct_relationship/3: majority of voting rights, tested exactly as 2 x held greater than total; the right to appoint or remove a majority of the body; the right to dominant influence by contract or articles; sole control of a majority of voting rights by agreement); whether two named enterprises belong to one single undertaking on the caller's list of relationships, directly or through one or more other enterprises (same_single_undertaking/3); and which enterprises form the single undertaking of one named enterprise (single_undertaking_members/4). Open-textured judgements (whether an entity is an enterprise, dominant influence, an unsure relationship) are handed to a lawyer as needs_legal_review; a missing fact is asked for as indeterminate. ## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "recitals_quoted_for_readings"

    **Why:** Recitals (4) and (5) are quoted because readings (3), (9), (13) and (14) rest on them, but they state reasons, not rules: no limb reads them and they carry no citation key. What they say beyond Art 2(2) (a natural person as an undertaking; a common public body alone does not link enterprises; the same Member State; the criteria selected from the definition of linked enterprises) is not formalised (open questions Q1 to Q4, Q9 and Q15).

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers follow Art 2(2) as printed; a lawyer who reads the recitals as narrowing or widening it gets a different group (open questions Q1 to Q4, Q9 and Q15).

??? info "scope_and_sector_exclusions"

    **Why:** Art 1 says to which aid the Regulation applies and excludes sectors and kinds of aid. It does not change whether enterprises are one single undertaking.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers never say whether aid falls within the Regulation.

??? info "other_definitions"

    **Why:** Art 2(1) defines agricultural products, primary production, processing, marketing, fishery and aquaculture products and financial intermediaries. No limb of this order uses those terms; Art 1 does.

    **Consequence for a user:** None for the three questions of this order.

??? info "ceiling_period_amount_mergers_and_splits"

    **Why:** Art 3 sets the ceiling of EUR 300 000 per Member State per single undertaking over any period of 3 years, says when aid is granted and how amounts count, and allocates aid on mergers, acquisitions and splits. This order only says which enterprises form the single undertaking that the ceiling applies to.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers never sum aid, test a ceiling or a period, or allocate aid after a merger or a split; a caller sums aid over the MEMBERS of single_undertaking_members under the de minimis rules.

??? info "gross_grant_equivalent_and_transparency"

    **Why:** Art 4 says how the gross grant equivalent of aid is calculated and which aid is transparent. No amount is read here.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers never value aid.

??? info "cumulation_with_other_aid"

    **Why:** Art 5 governs cumulation of de minimis aid with other aid. No amount is read here.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers never test cumulation.

??? info "check_before_grant_and_financial_intermediaries"

    **Why:** Art 6(4) lets a Member State grant new de minimis aid only after verifying that the total granted to the undertaking concerned stays within the Art 3(2) ceiling; Art 6(5) has financial intermediaries report the aid they received each quarter and fixes its date of granting as the last day of the quarter. Both turn on amounts and dates of aid, which this order does not read.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers never say whether a grant may be made; the undertaking concerned is the group that single_undertaking_members gives.

??? info "transitional_provisions"

    **Why:** Art 7 applies the Regulation to aid granted before its entry into force if the aid fulfils its conditions; deems aid granted from 1 January 2014 to 31 December 2023 that fulfils the conditions of Regulation (EU) No 1407/2013 exempt from notification; allows grants for 6 months after its period of validity; and sets a duty on the Member State until the central register is set up. These concern aid and its dates, not which enterprises form a single undertaking.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers never say whether aid granted at a given date falls under the Regulation.

??? info "entry_into_force_and_period_of_application"

    **Why:** Art 8 says the Regulation enters into force on 1 January 2024 and applies until 31 December 2030. This order takes no date and answers on Art 2(2) as pinned (reading (15)).

    **Consequence for a user:** A caller checks that the Regulation applies on the date of the aid; the answers do not.
