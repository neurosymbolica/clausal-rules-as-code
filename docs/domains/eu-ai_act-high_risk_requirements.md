??? info "intended_purpose_state_of_the_art_and_risk_management_in_article_8_1"

    **Why:** Art 8(1): 'taking into account their intended purpose as well as the generally acknowledged state of the art on AI and AI-related technologies' and the second sentence (the risk management system is taken into account when ensuring compliance) say how compliance is judged, with open words; the title carries no rule. The order reads only that the requirements apply (reading (4)).

    **Consequence for a user:** An answer requirement_applies does not say what compliance takes for the system.

??? info "products_under_annex_i_section_a_legislation"

    **Why:** Art 8(2): the provider is responsible for the full compliance of a product containing an AI system with the Union harmonisation legislation of Annex I, Section A, and may integrate testing, reporting, information and documentation into that legislation's procedures 'as appropriate'; that legislation is not pinned and the choice turns on an open word.

    **Consequence for a user:** The caller does not learn from this order how the product legislation and this Section combine.

??? info "content_of_the_risk_management_system"

    **Consequence for a user:** An answer requirement_applies does not say that the system meets the requirement.

??? info "steps_measures_and_testing_of_the_risk_management_system"

    **Consequence for a user:** The caller does not learn from this order what the risk management system must hold or whether it does.
