## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), consolidated text 02016R0679-20160504, Art 4(20) and Art 47(2), points (a) to (n). Given a profile of one set of policies, the party that adheres to them and the transfers they are for, the package answers: (A) whether the policies are binding corporate rules within the meaning of Art 4(20), read as seven elements (binding_corporate_rules_definition/3); (B) whether the rules specify everything Art 47(2) says binding corporate rules shall specify at least, the whole of each point, presence not adequacy except point (n) (binding_corporate_rules_content/3); (C) the state of each point (a) to (n) with its citation (binding_corporate_rules_content_checklist/2). The roles of controller and processor, the group element and the appropriateness of training are the caller's judgements; an unsure judgement goes to a lawyer (needs_legal_review).
