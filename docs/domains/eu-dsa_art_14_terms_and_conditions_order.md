## Scope

Whether a provider of one intermediary service meets each of the six duties of Article 14 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act) on its terms and conditions: Art 14(1) content, language and availability of the terms and conditions (including the rules of procedure of an internal complaint handling system the provider has); Art 14(2) informing recipients of a significant change; Art 14(3) explaining conditions and restrictions in a way minors can understand where the service is primarily directed at or predominantly used by minors; Art 14(4) diligent, objective and proportionate enforcement of restrictions with due regard to the rights of all parties; Art 14(5) the summary owed by designated very large online platforms and search engines; Art 14(6) publication in the official languages of every Member State of service by such platforms and engines. Each duty is answered separately; open-textured judgements are the caller's (unsure goes to a lawyer); the Commission designation under Art 33 enters only as an attested outside fact. Out of scope: Art 19 (it excludes micro and small enterprises only from Section 3), Art 20, Art 28, Art 33, the Charter, supervision and ...
