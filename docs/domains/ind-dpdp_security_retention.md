## Scope

The Data Fiduciary's duties under section 8 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 other than breach intimation, read with rules 1(4), 6 and 8 and the Third and Seventh Schedules of the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025: reasonable security safeguards (s 8(5) with rule 6(1)); completeness, accuracy and consistency (s 8(3)); erasure and causing the Data Processor to erase (s 8(7)(a) and (b)), held back by the one-year retention of rule 8(3) and displaced by retention required by other law; the deemed end of the specified purpose for the three Third Schedule classes (s 8(8) and (11) with rule 8(1)); the latest day for the notice before erasure (rule 8(2)); the last day of the one-year minimum retention (rule 8(3)); and the fixed lists of the rule 6(1) minimum safeguards and the Seventh Schedule purposes. Whether the caller is the Data Fiduciary, whether data is likely to be used for a decision or disclosed, whether other law requires retention and whether the specified purpose is no longer served are the caller's judgements, referred to a lawyer when unsure.
