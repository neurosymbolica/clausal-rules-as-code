## Scope

## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "swiss_information_timing"

    **Why:** DSG Art 19(5) sets when the information is given where the data are not collected from the data subject: at the latest one month after the controller received the data, or at the latest at a disclosure made before that month ends. The order computes no date (reading (6)).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says by when the information must be given.

??? info "swiss_impact_assessment_content"

    **Why:** DSG Art 22(3) sets what an impact assessment contains (a description of the planned processing, an assessment of the risks to the personality or the fundamental rights of the data subject, and the protective measures); the order says only whether an assessment is needed (reading (11)).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says whether an assessment contains what it must.

??? info "eu_automated_decisions_on_special_categories"

    **Why:** GDPR Art 22(4) bars decisions under Art 22(2) based on special categories of personal data unless Art 9(2)(a) or (g) applies and suitable measures are in place. A decision of Art 22(4) is a decision of Art 22(1), which this order reads (reading (14)); whether such a decision is permitted is not answered.

    **Consequence for a user:** No EU answer says whether a decision on special categories of data is permitted.

??? info "eu_impact_assessment_procedure"

    **Why:** GDPR Art 35(2) (advice of the data protection officer), (6) (consistency mechanism for the lists), (8) (codes of conduct taken into account), (9) (views of data subjects) and (11) (review) govern how an assessment is carried out and kept up; this order says only whether one is required (reading (15)).

    **Consequence for a user:** No EU answer says how an assessment must be carried out or reviewed.
