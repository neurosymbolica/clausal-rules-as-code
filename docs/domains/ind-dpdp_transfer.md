## Scope

Restrictions on the transfer of personal data outside India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 s 16 (s 16(1) the notified restriction, s 16(2) other laws saved) and rule 15 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025, with rule 1(4) for the day rule 15 starts (13 May 2027, calculated by the drafter). For ONE transfer of personal data by ONE Data Fiduciary to ONE country or territory outside India on ONE reference day, it answers for each restriction on its own whether it reaches the transfer: notified_destination (restricted_see_notification, not_restricted, not_in_force) and foreign_state_requirements (requirements_apply_see_order, not_restricted, not_in_force), or names the one missing key, or refers an unsure open-textured judgement to a lawyer. Out of scope and never guessed: the commencement notifications of the Act, any notification under s 16(1), any order under rule 15 and their terms, and any other law saved by s 16(2); the breach, consent, security, rights, children and Significant Data Fiduciary rules. Drafter's reading, not a lawyer's.
