## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "dpa_164a_facilitation_and_response_duties"

    **Why:** s.164A(2) requires the controller to facilitate complaints by steps such as an electronic complaint form; s.164A(4) requires it, without undue delay, to take appropriate steps to respond and to inform the complainant of the outcome; s.164A(5) says those steps include enquiries to the extent appropriate and informing the complainant about progress. These are open-textured duties ('steps such as', 'without undue delay', 'appropriate') with no fixed time; this small order does not formalise them. Quoted for context with the deferred marker.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says whether a controller facilitated, responded to or reported the outcome of a complaint as s.164A(2), (4) and (5) require.

??? info "dpa_165_commission_duties"

    **Why:** s.165(3) to (5) set the Commission's duties on a complaint (facilitating complaints, responding, informing of the outcome and of the rights under s.166, further information on request, investigating to the extent appropriate and informing of progress); s.165(7) defines 'foreign designated authority' used by s.165(5)(b). This order says only whether a complaint may be made to the Commission.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says what the Commission must do with a complaint.

??? info "duaa_103_article_57_amendments"

    **Why:** s.103(5) omits Art 57(1)(f) and Art 57(2) of the UK GDPR (the Commissioner's tasks on complaints); Art 57 is not pinned and is not asked by this order.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says what the Commission's tasks under Art 57 are.
