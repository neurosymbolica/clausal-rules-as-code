## Scope

The financial entity's duties of ICT change management under Article 17(1) and (2) of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1774 (RTS on ICT risk management under Article 15 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, DORA), with the rule of DORA Article 16(1), first subparagraph, that Articles 5 to 15 of DORA do not apply to the entities it lists (answered simplified_framework_entity). For ONE financial entity it decides which of the sixteen duties apply (thirteen elements of Art 17(1), always; the stringent testing of Art 17(2) and the involvement of parties, for central counterparties and central securities depositories after significant changes), whether all are performed as the caller judges, and which are outstanding. Out of scope: whether the entity is a financial entity within DORA (presupposed), the simplified framework of DORA Art 16 and Title III, DORA Art 9(4)(e) itself, the other articles of the Delegated Regulation, and every open-textured judgement (adequate, effective, stringent, as appropriate), which is the caller's or, when unsure, a lawyer's.

## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "simplified_framework_duties"

    **Why:** DORA Art 16(1), first subparagraph (Articles 5 to 15 of DORA do not apply to the entities it lists), is formalised as the gate of every query (reading (2)). Not formalised: who those entities are, which the caller states, and the lead words of the second subparagraph, which introduce the duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework; those are the subject of the order on proportionality and the simplified framework.

    **Consequence for a user:** simplified_framework_entity says only that Article 17 of the Delegated Regulation does not apply; it gives none of the duties the entity has instead.

??? info "simplified_framework_duty_list"

    **Why:** DORA Art 16(1), second subparagraph, points (a) to (h), list the duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework, and DORA Art 16(2) requires that framework to be documented and reviewed. They bind only the entities of DORA Article 16(1), for which every query answers simplified_framework_entity.

    **Consequence for a user:** The order does not check the simplified framework.
