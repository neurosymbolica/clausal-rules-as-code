## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "eu_safeguard_duty"

    **Why:** EU Art 22(3) requires the controller, in the cases of Art 22(2)(a) and (c), to implement suitable measures, at least the right to obtain human intervention, to express his or her point of view and to contest the decision. This order compares whether the decision may be taken; it does not compare the EU safeguard duty with UK Art 22C.

    **Consequence for a user:** No EU answer says whether the Art 22(3) measures are implemented.

??? info "law_enforcement_automated_decisions"

    **Why:** Sections 50A to 50D of the Data Protection Act 2018, substituted by section 80(3) of the 2025 Act, govern automated decisions in law-enforcement processing by competent authorities (Part 3). This order formalises the UK GDPR only.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer covers processing under Part 3 of the Data Protection Act 2018.
