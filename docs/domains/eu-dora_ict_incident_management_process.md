## Scope

For ONE financial entity presupposed to be within the scope of DORA, which duties of Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA) Article 17 (the ICT-related incident management process: paragraph 1, the two sentences of paragraph 2, and paragraph 3, points (a) to (f)) and Article 18(2) (the classification of cyber threats as significant) apply to it (all ten, always, the entities of Article 16(1) included), whether it has performed all of them, and which are outstanding, each duty being a tri-state judgement of the caller (true, false, unsure; unsure is referred to a lawyer). Out of scope: whether the entity is a financial entity within scope (Articles 2 and 3), the classification of one incident under Article 18(1) and its reporting (the order on incident reporting), the mandates of Article 18(3) and (4), the simplified framework of Article 16, proportionality under Article 4, Articles 10, 11, 13 and 14, the delegated regulations 2024/1772 and 2024/1774, and dates of application.

## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "incident_classification_criteria"

    **Why:** DORA Art 18(1) (the classification of ICT-related incidents and the criteria for their impact) is applied to each incident, with Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1772, in the order on incident reporting. Its lead words are quoted here because Art 17(3)(b) names its criteria (reading (10)); no key of this order reads them.

    **Consequence for a user:** Point (b) asks only whether the classification procedures are established, not how one incident is classified.

??? info "simplified_framework_and_its_entities"

    **Why:** DORA Art 16(1) and (2): who the entities of the simplified ICT risk management framework are, and that framework's duties. They are formalised in the order on proportionality and the simplified framework. Art 16(1), first subparagraph, is quoted here only for reading (2): its exemption names Articles 5 to 15, so it does not reach Article 17 or 18, and no key of this order reads it.

    **Consequence for a user:** Every answer holds for the entities of Article 16(1) as for any other financial entity; the order gives none of the duties of the simplified framework.

??? info "proportionality_in_chapter_ii"

    **Why:** DORA Art 4(1) applies the principle of proportionality to Chapter II, which this order does not formalise; it is read in the order on proportionality and the simplified framework.

    **Consequence for a user:** None for these answers.

??? info "proportionality_in_chapter_iii"

    **Why:** DORA Art 4(2) (proportionality in Chapters III, IV and V, Section I, 'as specifically provided for in the relevant rules of those Chapters') is quoted for reading (3): Article 17 and Article 18(2) provide nothing specific, so it removes no duty and adds no key here. Not formalised as a rule.

    **Consequence for a user:** No duty is removed for size; how far a duty goes for a small entity is part of the caller's judgement.
