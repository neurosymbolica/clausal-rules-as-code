## Scope

Two procedures of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 with the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025 (drafter's reading, not a lawyer's). (a) The appeal to the Appellate Tribunal against an order or direction of the Data Protection Board of India (s 29(1) to (3), (6) and (7) with rule 22(1) and (2)): whether an appeal lies and is filed within the sixty days of s 29(2) (appeal_period, a late appeal answered as needing sufficient cause, never as barred), what the filing must meet under rule 22 (appeal_filing_requirements) and the six-month target of s 29(6) with the duty of s 29(7) to record reasons (appeal_disposal_target). (b) The Central Government's call for information (s 36 with rule 23(1) and (2) and the Seventh Schedule): whether the recipient must furnish it (information_call_status) and whether rule 23(2) bars disclosure of the furnishing (non_disclosure_status). Rule 1(4) dates rules 22 and 23 (13 May 2027, a calculated date). The commencement of s 29 and s 36, the open-textured judgements and the identity of an intermediary or authorised person are stated by the caller; a call outside the Seventh Schedule and every unsure judgement go to a lawyer.
