## Scope

The duties that Articles 1 to 3 of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1774 (the RTS on ICT risk management tools, methods, processes and policies) place on ONE financial entity: taking its size, overall risk profile and complexity into account (Article 1, for Title II only), the general elements of its ICT security policies, procedures, protocols and tools (Article 2(1) and 2(2)) and its ICT risk management policies and procedures (Article 3, both paragraphs): twenty-seven duties, each a caller judgement (true, false or unsure), behind the gate of DORA Article 16(1), first subparagraph (an entity listed there applies the simplified framework of Title III instead, answered as simplified_framework_entity). Three queries: which duties are required, whether all are performed, and which are outstanding. An unsure judgement is handed to a lawyer (needs_legal_review, open_textured); no crisp test is invented. Out of scope: whether the entity is a financial entity within DORA (presupposed), the other articles of the RTS (Articles 4 to 42), the simplified framework and the qualification of the entities of DORA Article 16(1), the duties of DORA itself, and any date. ## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "simplified_framework_duties"

    **Why:** DORA Art 16(1), first subparagraph (Articles 5 to 15 of DORA do not apply to the entities it lists), is formalised as the gate of every query (reading (2)). Not formalised: who those entities are, which the caller states, and the lead words of the second subparagraph, which introduce the duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework; those are the subject of the order on proportionality and the simplified framework and of the order on Title III of the RTS.

    **Consequence for a user:** simplified_framework_entity says only that Articles 1 to 3 of the RTS, as Title II provisions, do not apply; it gives none of the duties the entity has instead.

??? info "simplified_framework_duty_list"

    **Why:** DORA Art 16(1), second subparagraph, points (a) to (h), list the duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework, and Art 16(2) requires that framework to be documented and reviewed. They bind only the entities of Article 16(1), for which every query answers simplified_framework_entity.

    **Consequence for a user:** The order does not check the simplified framework.
