## Scope

What a financial entity's report on the review of its ICT risk management framework must be and contain under Article 27 of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1774 (the RTS on ICT risk management tools, methods, processes and policies): the searchable electronic format of Article 27(1) and the items of Article 27(2), points (a) to (l), with the sentences of its second subparagraph for points (c) and (f). The report is the one of DORA Article 6(5), submitted to the competent authority upon its request (third sentence, the request key); the entities of DORA Article 16(1), first subparagraph, are outside Title II (the gate). Three questions about one financial entity and its report: which duties apply (ict_risk_framework_review_report_duties_required), whether the report meets all of them (ict_risk_framework_review_report_duties_complete), and which are outstanding (ict_risk_framework_review_report_duties_outstanding). Each item is a caller judgement (true, false or unsure); unsure goes to a lawyer as an open-textured question. Out of scope: whether the entity is a financial entity within DORA (Article 2), the review itself, the request and the duty to submit (DORA Article 6), ...

## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "report_on_review_upon_request"

    **Why:** DORA Art 6(5), third sentence (a report on the review of the ICT risk management framework is submitted to the competent authority upon its request), is the ground of the request key and of step (G4) of every query (reading (3)); the key is read with the key and meaning of the DORA order on ICT risk governance and framework. Not formalised here: the first two sentences, the documentation and review of the framework and its occasions, and its continuous improvement.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers do not say whether or when the framework was reviewed, or whether the report was submitted; they say what Article 27 requires of the report the authority requested.

??? info "ict_risk_management_framework"

    **Why:** DORA Art 6(1) to (4) and (6) to (10), with the points of paragraph 8, set the ICT risk management framework itself: its content, the management of ICT risk, the control function, internal audit, the digital operational resilience strategy and the options of paragraphs 9 and 10. No limb of this order reads them.

    **Consequence for a user:** complete and none_outstanding say nothing about the framework itself (open question (Q17)).

??? info "simplified_framework_duties"

    **Why:** DORA Art 16(1), first subparagraph (Articles 5 to 15 do not apply to the entities it lists), is formalised as the gate of every query (reading (2)). Not formalised: who those entities are, which the caller states, and the lead words of the second subparagraph, which introduce the duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework; those are the subject of the order on proportionality and the simplified framework.

    **Consequence for a user:** simplified_framework_entity says only that Article 27 does not apply; it gives none of the duties the entity has instead.

??? info "simplified_framework_duty_list"

    **Why:** DORA Art 16(1), second subparagraph, points (a) to (h), list the duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework, and Art 16(2) requires that framework to be documented and reviewed. They bind only the entities of Article 16(1), for which every query answers simplified_framework_entity.

    **Consequence for a user:** The order does not check the simplified framework.
