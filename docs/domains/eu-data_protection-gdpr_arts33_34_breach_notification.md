## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) Articles 33 and 34 (pinned consolidated text 02016R0679-20160504): whether a controller's notification of a personal data breach to the supervisory authority met Art 33(1) (72 hours counted under Regulation (EEC, Euratom) No 1182/71 Art 3 applied by analogy, without undue delay, reasons for a delay); which duties a controller or processor bears (Art 33(1), 33(2), 33(5), 34(1), 34(3), 34(4)); the calendar day and hour at which the 72-hour period ends; and whether the content of the notification (Art 33(3), 33(4)) and of the communication to the data subject (Art 34(1), 34(2)) is complete. Every risk, undue-delay, possibility, appropriateness, disproportionate-effort and clear-language assessment is a judgement the caller states; an unsure judgement is referred to a lawyer. Out of scope: which authority is competent (Art 55), whether a personal data breach occurred (Art 4(12)), the adequacy of security measures (Arts 24, 25, 32) and soft-law guidance.
