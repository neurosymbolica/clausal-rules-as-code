## Scope

Personal data breach notification under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) Articles 33 and 34. Decided here: (1) whether the controller's notification to the supervisory authority met Art 33(1) given the hours of the period begun at the notification (eligible, ineligible when the breach is unlikely to result in a risk, or notification_requirement_not_met for undue delay inside 72 hours or no reasons beyond them; a notification after 72 hours is never ineligible on lateness alone); (2) the duties owed: Art 33(1) notification, Art 33(2) processor notification, Art 33(5) documentation of every breach, and the Art 34 communication to the data subject with the Art 34(3) exemptions and the Art 34(4) decision of the supervisory authority; (3) the 72-hour period of Art 33(1), its last calendar day and the full UTC hour at which it expires, counted under Regulation (EEC, Euratom) No 1182/71 Art 3 applied by analogy and never extended for a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday; (4) the content of the notification against Art 33(3) and 33(4) and of the communication against Art 34(1) timing and 34(2).
