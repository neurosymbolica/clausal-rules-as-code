## Scope

The personal data breach duties of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 Articles 33 and 34 (pinned consolidated text 02016R0679-20160504): whether the controller's notification to the supervisory authority under Art 33(1) was in order (timing, undue delay, reasons for a delay beyond 72 hours); which duties arise for the controller (Art 33(1) notification, Art 33(5) documentation, Art 34(1) communication to the data subject, the Art 34(3) exemptions and the Art 34(4) decision of the supervisory authority) or for the processor (Art 33(2)); the calendar day and the full UTC hour at which the 72-hour period ends, applying Regulation (EEC, Euratom) No 1182/71 Art 3 by analogy; and whether a notification meets Art 33(3) and 33(4) and a communication meets Art 34(1) timing and Art 34(2). Every open-textured assessment (risk, high risk, undue delay, justified reasons, appropriate measures, possible, disproportionate effort, clear and plain language) is stated by the caller as a tri-state judgement and an unsure one is referred to a lawyer.
