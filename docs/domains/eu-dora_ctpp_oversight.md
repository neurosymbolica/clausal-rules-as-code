## Scope

The oversight framework of Articles 32 to 35 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA) for ONE ICT third-party service provider designated as critical (THE PROVIDER): the duties those Articles place on THE PROVIDER (Art 35(1), point (d), closing words; Art 35(5)); the powers the Lead Overseer has in respect of it (Art 35(1), points (a) to (d) and (d)(iv)); whether its two windows to answer the Lead Overseer are open (Art 33(4), fourth sentence; Art 35(3), second sentence); when the ground for a periodic penalty payment is met (Art 35(6)); the outer period and the daily ceiling of such a payment (Art 35(7); Art 35(8), first sentence); and when competent authorities need the Lead Overseer's agreement for a measure concerning THE PROVIDER (Art 33(5)). ## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "oversight_plan_adoption"

    **Why:** Art 33(4), third and fourth sentences (the draft oversight plan is communicated to THE PROVIDER, which may submit a reasoned statement within 15 calendar days of its receipt), are formalised (reading (6)). Not formalised here: the adoption of the individual oversight plan on the basis of the assessment and in coordination with the JON, its content (the annual oversight objectives and main oversight actions) and its yearly communication.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says what the oversight plan contains or when it is adopted.

??? info "recommendation_areas"

    **Why:** Art 35(1), point (d)(i) to (iii): the areas of recommendation (ICT security and quality requirements, conditions and terms of the services, planned subcontracting), each resting on what the Lead Overseer deems relevant or deems to trigger risks. They are the Lead Overseer's assessment; point (d) is listed as issue_recommendations without them (reading (3)). All three are quoted for readings (3) and (4) only.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says what a recommendation should contain.

??? info "consultation_before_powers"

    **Why:** Art 35(3), second sentence (THE PROVIDER's opportunity to provide information within 30 calendar days before recommendations), is formalised (reading (6)). Not formalised here: its first sentence, the Lead Overseer's consultation of the Oversight Forum before exercising the powers of Art 35(1).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says whether the Oversight Forum has been consulted.

??? info "penalty_amount_within_ceiling"

    **Why:** Art 35(8), first sentence (the ceiling of 1 % of the average daily worldwide turnover of the preceding business year), is formalised (reading (10)). Not formalised here: the date stipulated in the decision from which the amount is calculated, the criteria the Lead Overseer takes into account when determining the amount (points (a) to (c)), and its consultation within the JON.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answer gives the ceiling only, never the amount the Lead Overseer sets.

??? info "penalty_enforcement_and_procedure"

    **Why:** Art 35(9) (penalty payments are administrative and enforceable under the civil procedure of the Member State of the inspection; the amounts go to the Union budget), Art 35(10) (the Lead Overseer discloses every periodic penalty payment unless disclosure would seriously jeopardise the financial markets or cause disproportionate damage) and Art 35(11) (the right to be heard, the rights of the defence and access to the file; quoted for reading (8)). They govern the authorities' procedure and enforcement.

    **Consequence for a user:** penalty_decision_required says only that the ground of Art 35(6) is met (open question (Q8)).
