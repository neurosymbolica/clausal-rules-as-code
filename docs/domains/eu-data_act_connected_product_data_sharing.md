## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 (the Data Act), Chapter II: whether the design duty of Art 3(1) is met, whether the pre-contract information duty of Art 3(2) (purchase, rent or lease of a connected product) or Art 3(3) (provision of a related service) is met, whether a data holder's duty to make readily available data accessible to the user arises under Art 4 (with the security restriction of Art 4(2), the personal-data bar of Art 4(12) and the trade-secret grounds of Art 4(6) to 4(8)), and whether its duty to share such data with a third party arises under Art 5 (with the testing-data exception of Art 5(2), the gatekeeper exclusion of Art 5(3), the personal-data bar of Art 5(7) and the trade-secret grounds of Art 5(9) to 5(11)); read with the Art 2 definitions (5), (6), (12), (13), (15), (16) and (17), the small enterprise exemption of Art 7(1) and the application dates of Art 50, second and third paragraphs. An answer says whether a duty arises or a ground applies, not whether data were made available in the right manner or in time.
