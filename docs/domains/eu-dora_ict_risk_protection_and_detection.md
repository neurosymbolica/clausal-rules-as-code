## Scope

For ONE financial entity presupposed to be within the scope of Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA): which of the duties of protection and prevention (Article 9) and of detection (Article 10) apply to it, whether it has performed all of them, and which are outstanding, with the rule of Article 16(1), first subparagraph, that Articles 5 to 15 do not apply to the entities it lists (answered simplified_framework_entity). Each duty is a caller judgement (true, false, unsure); an unsure judgement that matters is referred to a lawyer. Out of scope: whether the entity is a financial entity within scope (the order on scope and entity types), the simplified ICT risk management framework of Article 16(1), second subparagraph, (2) and (3) (the order on proportionality and the simplified framework), Articles 5 to 8 and 11 to 15 (their own orders), Articles 17 and 25, and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1774. Every reading is the drafter's reading, not a lawyer's.

## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "simplified_framework_duties"

    **Why:** Art 16(1), first subparagraph (Articles 5 to 15 do not apply to the entities it lists), is formalised as the gate of every query (reading (2)). Not formalised: who those entities are, which the caller states, and the lead words of the second subparagraph, which introduce the duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework; those are the subject of the order on proportionality and the simplified framework.

    **Consequence for a user:** simplified_framework_entity says only that Articles 9 and 10 do not apply; it gives none of the duties the entity has instead.

??? info "simplified_framework_duty_list"

    **Why:** Art 16(1), second subparagraph, points (a) to (h), list the duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework, and Art 16(2) requires that framework to be documented and reviewed. They bind only the entities of Article 16(1), for which every query answers simplified_framework_entity.

    **Consequence for a user:** The order does not check the simplified framework.
