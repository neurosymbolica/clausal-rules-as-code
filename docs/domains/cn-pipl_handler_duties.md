## Scope

For ONE processing activity of the caller on ONE reference day: which duties the Personal Information Protection Law of the PRC (2021 adoption text, pinned npc.gov.cn page, formalised from the Chinese text) lays on a personal information handler under Chapter V (Art 51 compliance measures; Art 52(1) person in charge and Art 52(2) publishing and reporting the contact details; Art 53 dedicated body or representative of a handler outside the territory, read with Art 3(2); Art 54 compliance audit; Art 55 impact assessment and Art 56(2) keeping the report and records at least three years; Art 57 remedial measures and notices on leakage, tampering or loss, with the Art 57(2) permission not to notify individuals; Art 58 large platform obligations) and on a party processing on entrustment (Art 59), with Art 73 items (1) and (2) and the Art 74 in-force test (1 November 2021). Each answer says whether a duty is OWED, never whether it is MET; duty_contents lists what Arts 51, 56(1), 57(1) and 58 name. ## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "territorial_reach_article_3_1"

    **Why:** Article 3(1) says the Law applies to processing within the territory; no limb reads it: whether the Law reaches the processing is the caller's premise (reading (6), Q9).

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers hold only where the Law reaches the processing; the order never answers whether it does under Article 3(1).

??? info "officer_quantity_measure"

    **Why:** Whether the caller reaches the quantity of Article 52(1) is a crisp caller statement (reaches_officer_quantity, Q4).

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers rely on the caller's statement; the order holds no number.

??? info "other_measures_and_cases_prescribed_by_law"

    **Why:** Article 51 item (6) (other measures prescribed by laws and administrative regulations) is listed as an item only; Article 3(2) item (3) (other cases prescribed by laws or administrative regulations) is the crisp caller statement other_case_prescribed_by_law (Q14, Q8).

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers never say which other measures or cases those laws prescribe.

??? info "pipl_articles_outside_the_pin"

    **Why:** The definition of sensitive personal information, the rules on entrusted processing, making personal information public, image collection in public places and separate consent, and the cross-border articles are not pinned in this order (Q17, Q6).

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers rely on the caller's statements (processes_sensitive_personal_information, entrusts_processing, processes_on_entrustment, makes_personal_information_public, provides_outside_china); the cross-border duties are answered by the separate order on cross-border transfer.

??? info "later_amendments"

    **Why:** Whether a later amendment changed the pinned articles is not established (Q1).

    **Consequence for a user:** The caller states it through pinned_articles_unamended_on_reference_date.
