## Scope

For ONE AI system and its provider (and, for the registration of Art 49(3), one deployer of it), under Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act) as consolidated on 27 July 2026 (amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744): which conformity assessment procedure Art 43(1) to (3) gives the system; who acts as notified body for the procedure of Annex VII (Art 43(1), third subparagraph); whether a change asks for a new conformity assessment (Art 43(4), Art 3(23)); which EU declaration of conformity Art 47(1) and (3) asks for and whether it must still be kept ten years on (Art 47(1)); which of five elements of the CE marking Art 48 asks for; which registration Art 49 asks of the provider or a deployer; and the item lists of Annexes VI, VII (points 3.1 and 4.2), VIII and IX and of Art 49(4). Each answer is judged at its date (Art 113(c)(i) and (ii)) and through the transitions of Art 111(1) and (2). Out of scope: whether the system is an AI system, whether the Regulation applies (Art 2), who holds which role (order scope-and-roles), whether the system is high-risk (Art 6; the caller gives the classification keys), whether a procedure, declaration, marking or registration was made ...

## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "annex_vii_points_notified_bodies_and_product_rules_in_article_43_3"

    **Why:** Art 43(3) says which points of Annex VII apply in the procedure of the harmonisation legislation, gives notified bodies notified under that legislation the power to assess conformity on conditions, sets the designation deadline of 28 January 2028, and says that the classification does not affect the manufacturer's choice of procedure; the order formalises only the route and the option of the fifth sentence (reading (5)).

    **Consequence for a user:** An answer naming the procedure of the harmonisation legislation does not say which points of Annex VII apply or which notified body may act.

??? info "form_identification_and_copy_of_the_declaration"

    **Why:** Art 47(1) asks for a written machine readable, physical or electronically signed declaration that identifies the system, and for a copy upon request; Art 47(3), second sentence, says what the single declaration contains. The order formalises which declaration (Art 47(1), (3)) and the ten years (reading (8), reading (9)).

    **Consequence for a user:** An answer naming a declaration does not say what form or content it needs.

??? info "content_translation_and_responsibility_of_the_declaration"

    **Why:** Art 47(2) (the declaration states that the system meets Section 2, holds the information of Annex V, which is not pinned, and is translated into a language the authorities understand) and Art 47(4) (the provider assumes responsibility and keeps the declaration up to date as appropriate) set content and continuing duties no query answers.

    **Consequence for a user:** The caller reads them beside an answer on the declaration.

??? info "manner_of_the_ce_marking"

    **Why:** Art 48(1) (the general principles of Art 30 of Regulation (EC) No 765/2008, not pinned), the condition of easy access in Art 48(2), the words 'visibly, legibly and indelibly' and the choice between packaging and accompanying documentation 'as appropriate' in Art 48(3), and the second and third sentences of Art 48(4) (who affixes the identification number; promotional material) are not formalised (reading (10)).

    **Consequence for a user:** An answer marking_element_required does not say how the element is affixed or shown.

??? info "timing_of_the_registrations_and_access_to_the_secure_section"

    **Why:** The time words of Art 49(1) to (3) ('Before placing on the market or putting into service', 'Before putting into service or using') the words 'as applicable' in the lead of Art 49(4) and its last sentence (only the Commission and the national authorities of Art 74(8) have access to the restricted sections) are not formalised (reading (11)).

    **Consequence for a user:** An answer naming a registration does not say by when it is made or who may see it.

??? info "large_scale_it_systems_evaluation_in_article_111_1"

    **Why:** The second sentence of Art 111(1) (the requirements are taken into account in the evaluation of each large-scale IT system) binds those who evaluate the systems; the list of Annex X that the first sentence names is not in the pin (reading (4)).

    **Consequence for a user:** The caller gives the key on Annex X from the list it holds.

??? info "annex_x_list_of_legal_acts"

    **Why:** Art 111(1) applies to components of the large-scale IT systems established by the legal acts listed in Annex X; Annex X is not pinned, so the order cannot list them and reads the key component_of_an_annex_x_large_scale_it_system_placed_on_the_market_or_put_into_service_before_2_august_2027 as the caller gives it (reading (4), Q16).

    **Consequence for a user:** The caller decides from Annex X whether the system is such a component.

??? info "entry_into_force_and_general_date_of_article_113"

    **Why:** The article unit of Art 113 is cited with point (c) at the date steps; of its text only the general date of the second paragraph (read into reading (3)) and the lead words of the third paragraph (which introduce point (c)) are read; the entry into force in the first paragraph is not formalised.

    **Consequence for a user:** None for these answers beyond the dates of reading (3).

??? info "conditions_in_the_registration_lists"

    **Why:** The lead words of Annex VIII Sections A, B and C and of Annex IX ('shall be provided and thereafter kept up to date'), 'where applicable' in Section A points 3, 8 and 9, Section B point 3 and Section C point 5, the exception in Section A point 12 (no electronic instructions for points 1, 6 and 7 of Annex III) and 'optional' in Section A point 13 are not formalised: the lists name what the Annex lists (reading (12)).

    **Consequence for a user:** An answer of listed_items/3 lists the points; it does not say which apply in the case or check an entry.
