## Scope

The ICT risk governance and framework duties of ONE financial entity under Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA): the internal governance and control framework and the duties of the management body (Article 5), the ICT risk management framework, its control function, review, internal audit, follow-up and the digital operational resilience strategy (Article 6(1) to (8)), the ICT systems, protocols and tools (Article 7) and the identification of functions, assets, sources of ICT risk and dependencies (Article 8), with Article 16(1), first subparagraph, read only for the rule that Articles 5 to 15 do not apply to the entities it lists. Three questions: which of the thirty-four duties apply (ict_risk_governance_duties_required), whether all are performed (ict_risk_governance_duties_complete), and which are outstanding (ict_risk_governance_duties_outstanding). ## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "simplified_framework_duties_not_formalised"

    **Why:** Art 16(1) is PARTLY formalised. Its first subparagraph is read in one respect only: that Articles 5 to 15 do not apply to the entities it lists, which the caller states in the key financial_entity_is_article_16_1_entity, and the answer simplified_framework_entity cites it for that (reading (3)). Not formalised: which entities it lists, and the lead words of its second subparagraph ('Without prejudice to the first subparagraph, the entities listed in the first subparagraph shall:'), which introduce the duties of the simplified ICT risk management framework.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answer simplified_framework_entity says only that the thirty-four duties of this order do not apply; it never lists the duties of the simplified framework (open question (Q1)).

??? info "simplified_framework_duties_and_review"

    **Why:** Art 16(1), points (a) to (h), and Art 16(2) set the duties and the review of the simplified ICT risk management framework of the entities of Art 16(1). This order answers only that Articles 5 to 8 do not apply to those entities (reading (3)).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer of this order lists or checks a duty of the simplified framework.
