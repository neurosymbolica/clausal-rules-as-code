## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act), consolidated text 02024R1689-20260727. Decided here: whether, on a given assessment date, the actor named as provider of one AI system must establish and document a post-market monitoring system based on a post-market monitoring plan that is part of the technical documentation (Art 72(1) and 72(3), first and second sentences), judged at the Chapter III application dates of Art 113, third paragraph, point (c)(i) and (ii), with the transitional rules for Annex X large-scale IT system components (Art 111(1)) and for systems already placed on the market or put into service (Art 111(2)); and whether that provider has the choice of Art 72(4) to integrate into a post-market monitoring system and plan already established under Union harmonisation legislation of Annex I Section A or Union financial services law. ## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "proportionality_of_the_post_market_monitoring_system"

    **Why:** Art 72(1): the words 'in a manner that is proportionate to the nature of the AI technologies and the risks of the high-risk AI system' are an open word the order does not judge; the order reads who must establish and document the system (reading (5), Q18).

    **Consequence for a user:** An answer duty_applies does not say what a proportionate system is, nor that the provider has one.

??? info "content_of_the_post_market_monitoring_system"

    **Why:** Art 72(2) says what the system does: actively and systematically collect, document and analyse relevant data on the performance of high-risk AI systems throughout their lifetime, which allow the provider to evaluate their continuous compliance with Chapter III, Section 2; where relevant, an analysis of the interaction with other AI systems; and it does not cover sensitive operational data of deployers which are law-enforcement authorities. No limb reads it (reading (5), Q19).

    **Consequence for a user:** An answer duty_applies does not say what the system must collect or analyse.

??? info "guidance_and_template_of_the_commission"

    **Why:** Art 72(3), third sentence: the Commission, taking utmost account of the opinion of the Board, adopts guidance, including a template, on the post-market monitoring plan by 2 September 2027. It binds the Commission; the order reads the first and second sentences (reading (12), Q15).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says whether the guidance and template have been adopted.

??? info "manner_and_extent_of_the_integration"

    **Why:** Art 72(4): the words 'as appropriate', which elements are 'necessary', the purposes 'in order to ensure consistency, avoid duplications and minimise additional burdens' and the use of the template are not formalised; the order reads whether the choice is open (reading (9), Q17, Q15).

    **Consequence for a user:** An answer integration_available does not say how far the integration must go or how it is done.

??? info "evaluation_of_large_scale_it_systems"

    **Why:** Art 111(1), second sentence (the requirements are taken into account in the evaluation of each large-scale IT system and where its legal acts are replaced or amended) binds those who evaluate the systems; the order reads the first sentence only (reading (7)).

    **Consequence for a user:** None for a provider's answer.

??? info "entry_into_force_and_general_date_of_article_113"

    **Why:** Art 113: the entry into force and the general date of the second paragraph (2 August 2026), the date of Art 72 itself; no step reads it, because the classification dates of point (c) are later and decide (reading (2), Q2, Q1).

    **Consequence for a user:** None for these answers, on the reading stated; read the other way the general date would decide.
