## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "regulation_611_2013_other_paragraphs"

    **Why:** Commission Regulation (EU) No 611/2013 Art 2(1) (notify all breaches), (3) (information in phases), (4) (the secure electronic means) and (5) (omitted) are not formalised; Art 2(2) is read for the 72 hours and its counting (reading (B4)).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says whether information may follow in phases or how the notification is sent.

??? info "regulation_5a_subscriber_notification_content_and_inventory"

    **Why:** PECR reg 5A(3) and (5) to (7) (notifying the subscriber or user and its exemption), (4) (content of the notification to the Commission), (8) (inventory) and (9) (omitted) are not formalised: this order answers only when the notification to the Commission is in time (reading (B5)).

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer says whether the subscriber or user had to be notified, or whether a notification or the inventory has the required content.
