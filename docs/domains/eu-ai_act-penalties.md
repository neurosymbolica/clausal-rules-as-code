## Scope

The HIGHEST administrative fine Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act), in its consolidated text valid from 27 July 2026 (as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744), allows for an infringement: maximum_fine/4 gives the cap of Art 99(3) to (5) with the SME rule of Art 99(6) and the SMC rule of Art 99(6a), the referral of public authorities and bodies to Member State rules under Art 99(8), and the caps of Art 100(2) and (3) for Union institutions, bodies, offices and agencies; general_purpose_ai_model_maximum_fine/4 gives the cap of Art 101(1) for providers of general-purpose AI models, handing the Commission's finding of intent or negligence to a lawyer when the caller is unsure. Both read the date of the infringement against Art 113 (points (a) and (b) and the general date). Out of scope: the fine for a case (the factors of Art 99(7), Art 100(1) points (a) to (g) and the last sentences of Art 101(1)), national penalties under Art 99(1), procedure and reporting, the earlier text of the Regulation for an infringement before 27 July 2026, whether an infringement occurred, and the definitions of SME, SMC, undertaking and provider, which are the caller's inputs.

## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "factors_for_deciding_on_a_fine"

    **Why:** Art 99(7) and Art 100(1) points (a) to (g) list the circumstances taken into account when deciding whether to impose a fine and on its amount (gravity, duration, cooperation, intent or negligence, size, budget and others). The Regulation gives the factors and no weights, so this order does not compute a fine; it gives only the cap.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers never say whether a fine should be imposed or what fine is appropriate, only the highest fine the law allows.

??? info "fixing_the_amount_of_a_commission_fine"

    **Why:** The last sentences of Art 101(1) (in fixing the amount regard is had to the nature, gravity and duration of the infringement, proportionality and appropriateness, and commitments under Art 93(3) or codes of practice under Art 56) sit in the same unit as the cap, which the order reads; the factors are not formalised.

    **Consequence for a user:** general_purpose_ai_model_maximum_fine gives only the cap of Art 101(1), never the amount the Commission should fix.

??? info "application_dates_of_other_chapters"

    **Why:** Art 113 third paragraph point (c) (Chapter III Sections 1 to 3 from 2 December 2027 or 2 August 2028) and point (d) (Arts 102 to 110 from 27 July 2026) date provisions outside this order; no limb reads them. Whether an obligation in the list of Art 99(4) applied on DATE is part of whether there was an infringement, which is the caller's input.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers do not check whether the infringed obligation itself applied on DATE, except for the Art 5 prohibitions of Art 113 point (a).

??? info "earlier_text_of_the_regulation"

    **Why:** For an infringement before 27 July 2026 the text before Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 governs; it is not pinned, so the answer is earlier_version_applies.

    **Consequence for a user:** The answers give no cap for an infringement dated before 27 July 2026.
