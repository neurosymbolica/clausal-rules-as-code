## Scope

For one provider of one online platform service and, for Art 28(2), one practice of presenting advertisements to one recipient (or recipients treated alike), this domain answers four questions under Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act), original act, English text: (1) whether Section 3 of Chapter III applies to the provider under the micro and small enterprise exclusion of Art 19(1) and its derogation for designated very large online platforms in Art 19(2); (2) whether the obligation of Art 28(1) to put in place appropriate and proportionate measures for a high level of privacy, safety and security of minors applies; (3) whether the provider meets that obligation; (4) whether presenting advertisements based on profiling of a recipient the provider is aware with reasonable certainty is a minor is prohibited by Art 28(2).
