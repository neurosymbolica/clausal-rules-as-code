## Scope

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) Art 4 definitions of the roles, the special data kinds, the establishments and the authority notions: points 4(7) controller, 4(8) processor, 4(13) genetic data, 4(14) biometric data, 4(15) data concerning health, 4(16) main establishment, 4(18) enterprise, 4(19) group of undertakings, 4(22) supervisory authority concerned, 4(23) cross-border processing and 4(24) relevant and reasoned objection. Personal data (4(1)) is carried as one judgement key. Decided here: whether each defined term is met on a profile of facts, so that every data-protection order reads it the same way. Out of scope: any obligation that applies, which supervisory authority leads, and points 4(17), 4(20), 4(21) (deferred) and the other Art 4 points (sibling order gdpr-common-definitions-data-and-processing).
