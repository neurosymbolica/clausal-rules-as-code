## Scope

The contract side of the management of ICT third-party risk under Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA): for ONE contractual arrangement on the use of ICT services between a financial entity and an ICT third-party service provider, which elements the contract must contain (contract_provisions_required), whether it contains all of them (contract_provisions_sufficient) and which it lacks (contract_provisions_missing), under Art 28(7)(a) to (d) (termination circumstances), Art 30(1) (one written document), Art 30(2)(a) to (i) (elements of every arrangement) and Art 30(3)(a) to (f) (additional elements for ICT services supporting critical or important functions, point (e) split in two, with the microenterprise derogation from point (e) in the closing words of Art 30(3)). Every element, and whether the arrangement supports a critical or important function, is the caller's judgement (true, false or unsure); an unsure judgement that can change the answer is referred to a lawyer. Out of scope: whether the entity is a financial entity within scope and the arrangement one on the use of ICT services (presupposed, Articles 2 and 3), the general principles, strategy, register of information, ...

## Provisions deferred, and what follows

??? info "general_principles"

    **Why:** Art 28(1) requires financial entities to manage ICT third-party risk within their ICT risk management framework, to remain fully responsible for their obligations (point (a)) and to apply the principle of proportionality (point (b)). It is quoted for reading (5) (proportionality does not remove an element of Art 30); it states principles with no condition and no output this order computes.

    **Consequence for a user:** No answer turns on it; proportionality removes no element in this order (open question (Q5)).

??? info "strategy_on_ict_third_party_risk"

    **Why:** Art 28(2) requires a strategy on ICT third-party risk with a policy on ICT services supporting critical or important functions, reviewed by the management body; microenterprises and the entities of Article 16(1) are exempt. It is quoted for reading (5) only (the microenterprise exemption is from the strategy, not from Art 30). The policy content is specified by Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1773, which is named and not formalised.

    **Consequence for a user:** The order does not say whether the entity needs or has a strategy or policy.

??? info "register_of_information"

    **Why:** Art 28(3) requires a register of information on all contractual arrangements on the use of ICT services, yearly reporting, the register on request and timely information on planned arrangements for critical or important functions. The templates are in Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2956, which is named and not formalised.

    **Consequence for a user:** The order does not check the register or the reporting.

??? info "assessments_and_conditions_before_contracting"

    **Why:** Art 28(4) lists the assessments before entering into an arrangement (whether it supports a critical or important function, supervisory conditions, risks including concentration risk, due diligence, conflicts of interest) and Art 28(5) allows arrangements only with providers that comply with appropriate information security standards. These are duties and conditions on the entity before contracting, not contents of the contract; a later order may take them.

    **Consequence for a user:** sufficient and none_missing say nothing about these duties (reading (9)).

??? info "exercise_of_audit_rights"

    **Why:** Art 28(6) governs how access, inspection and audit rights are exercised (a risk-based frequency and areas, commonly accepted audit standards, auditor skills for complex services). It is a duty in exercising rights, not a content of the contract.

    **Consequence for a user:** None for these answers.

??? info "exit_strategies_and_plans"

    **Why:** Art 28(8) requires exit strategies for ICT services supporting critical or important functions, the ability to exit without the harms of points (a) to (c), tested and reviewed exit plans, transition plans and contingency measures. These are duties on the entity; the matching contract element is Art 30(3)(f), which this order formalises.

    **Consequence for a user:** sufficient and none_missing say nothing about the exit strategy or plans (reading (9)).

??? info "ict_concentration_risk_and_subcontracting_considerations"

    **Why:** Article 29 requires a preliminary assessment of ICT concentration risk for arrangements on critical or important functions (providers not easily substitutable; several arrangements with the same or closely connected providers) and considerations on subcontracting, insolvency law, data recovery, third-country providers and long subcontracting chains. These are duties to weigh and consider before contracting, not contents of the contract.

    **Consequence for a user:** sufficient and none_missing say nothing about these duties (reading (9)).
