## Scope

Article 91 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), from the pinned text only. church_rules_continuation/4 answers whether Art 91(1) lets the comprehensive rules a church or a religious association or community applied in a Member State at the time of entry into force of the Regulation continue to apply, provided that they are brought into line with the Regulation. church_supervision/4 answers, for the same profile, whether Art 91(2) is engaged and, if it is, whether the church or association is subject to an independent supervisory authority, or whether a specific authority is permitted (it is attested to fulfil the conditions of Chapter VI) or not. Out of scope and handed to the caller or a lawyer: the content of any church rule or Member State law; whether the rules are comprehensive and brought into line (tri-state judgements, unsure refers to a lawyer); the time of entry into force (Art 99) and the conditions of Chapter VI, both taken only as attested outside facts; the definitions of Art 4.
