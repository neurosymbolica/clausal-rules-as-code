# Building on it

This release is a foundation, not a finished system. It shows a method, applied end to end to
a small number of narrow legal questions, with the evidence attached. What it would take to go
further is fairly clear, and most of it is work that needs people other than programmers.

## What is already in place

- A method that takes a legal question from a commissioning specification to an accepted,
  tested formalization, with every step checked.
- Domains from three different areas of EU law (anti-money-laundering, state aid, public
  procurement), showing that the method is not tied to one kind of rule.
- Tooling that anyone can run: tests, citation and parameter checks, provision tracking and the
  side-by-side reports.
- An open licence and an open engine.

## What a next stage would add

- **Independent legal review** of each domain's readings, deferrals and test cases, with the
  corrections recorded as described in [Lawyers in the loop](lawyers.md).
- **Coverage of whole instruments** rather than single questions, and of the national measures
  that transpose a directive.
- **Further official languages**, quoted from the same pinned sources.
- **Mapping to AKN4EU** identifiers as the Publications Office's migration progresses.
- **Use in practice**: an administration, supervisor or business applying a domain to real
  cases and reporting where the reading or the scope falls short.

## Further domains in preparation

The wider corpus behind this release holds more domains across EU and other jurisdictions,
which are published as they pass the same checks. A domain is added to a release only when every
check on the [status page](status.md) passes for it.
