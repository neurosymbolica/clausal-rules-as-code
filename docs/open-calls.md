# Open calls

The project welcomes people who would like to work on any of the following. Each is useful on
its own; none requires committing to more than you choose.

## Review of EU law

Lawyers with expertise in anti-money-laundering, state aid or public procurement law, to review
a domain's reading of the provisions: its operative readings, its deferrals and the consequence
stated for each, and its test cases. A review can be as small as one domain. Findings are
recorded as changes with a test, so the review is visible and lasting.

## Official languages

Readers of the official languages of the EU who can check quotations of the pinned text in their
language, so that a domain can carry its citations in more than one language.

## Practitioners and administrations

Public administrations, supervisors, contracting authorities, compliance teams and their
advisers who would like to try a domain against cases they know, and tell us where it is right,
where it is wrong and where its scope stops short of what they need.

## Standards and research

Researchers and standards bodies working on legal document standards (AKN4EU, ELI), Rules as
Code, legal informatics or logic programming, on mapping the formalizations to those standards
and on the method itself.

## Software

Developers interested in the engine, the tooling, or integrating domains into other systems.

## Getting in touch

Write to mikeamycoder@gmail.com, saying which of these interests you. Code contributions can be
merged once the contributor licence agreement is ready; until then, issues and discussion are
welcome on the repository.
