# us/texas_tdpsa

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Tex. Bus. & Com. Code ch. 541 (Texas Data Privacy and Security Act): for one person or controller the caller describes, whether the chapter applies (Sec. 541.002(a), (b); the SBA small-business determination is an attested outside fact, never computed); the last day to respond to a consumer request or to tell the consumer the controller declines to act (Sec. 541.052(b), (c)); the last day of the once-extended response period or that no extension is available (Sec. 541.052(b)); the last day to answer an appeal in writing (Sec. 541.053(c)); the last day of the 30-day cure period after the attorney general's written notice (Sec. 541.154); on a reference day, whether the cure period is running, the attorney general may bring an action, or no action may be brought for a cured violation (Secs. 541.154, 541.155(a)); and the maximum civil penalty in whole US dollars (Sec. 541.155(a)). Periods are calendar days from the event, the event day not counted, never moved for weekends or holidays (Tex. Gov't Code 311.014 not pinned). Out of scope (handed to the caller or a lawyer): whether conducting business in Texas or the necessity of an extension holds (open-textured judgements), the SBA size ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 235 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Citation records carry a label and a link to Texas Statutes (Texas Legislature Online); no statute text is quoted and none is pinned in this folder (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
