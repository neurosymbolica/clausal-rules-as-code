query(Type) :- what the reasoner can answer.
Demo: these rules haven't been reviewed by a lawyer yet. Not legal advice. This is not a product, it is just a technology showcase for exploring ideas and testing.
The same formalized rules answer many kinds of question, not just “yes or no”. Each card below runs a ready-made case straight on the rules engine: no language model is involved, so the answer is exact and the same every time. Press
Run
to see the answer, the exact query and the engine’s result.
