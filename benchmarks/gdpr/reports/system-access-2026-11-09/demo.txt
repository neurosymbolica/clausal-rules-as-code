This demo covers five areas of Singapore law: small-company audit exemption, GST registration, PDPA data-breach notification, corporate service provider registration, and Payment Services Act licences.
What is this?
How it works
A language model (neural network) translates natural language into logic queries. A rules engine, holding the law as code in a symbolic logic program, runs the query and produces an answer. Combining the two is called neurosymbolic AI.
Limits
This is a demo. Its rules haven't been reviewed by a lawyer yet, so it isn't legal advice.
