Austrian hospital with unclear public mandate

q1: Must this operator designate a DPO under Article 37(1)(c), despite the unresolved public/private classification? Explain the relevant facts and next steps.
q2: Can you determine from these facts whether Austria's DSG section 30(5) excludes GDPR fines for this operator? Identify the missing legal and factual checks.
