# uk/uk_automated_decision_making

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

UK law in force, not a proposal; drafter's reading, not a lawyer's. UK GDPR Chapter III Section 4A (automated individual decision-making), as substituted for the old Art 22 by section 80(1) of the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, for a decision taken on or after 5 February 2026 (S.I. 2026/82 reg 2(j) and (z8), with reg 5), under the legislation.gov.uk 'Latest available (Revised)' text fetched 2026-11-15. Decided here: whether a decision is a significant decision based solely on automated processing (Art 22A); whether Art 22B lets it be taken based solely on automated processing (special categories of personal data, Art 22B(1) to (3); reliance on Art 6(1)(ea), Art 22B(4)); whether Art 22C requires safeguards and, if so, whether the four measures of Art 22C(2) are in place; and, for comparison, this order's own reading of EU GDPR Art 22(1), (2)(a) to (c) and (4) (Formex consolidation 02016R0679-20160504) set beside the UK Art 22B answer on the same profile. Open-textured judgements (meaningful human involvement, legal or similarly significant effect, explicit consent, necessity, authorisation by law, Art 9(2) points, the safeguard measures) are the caller's: unsure goes to a lawyer ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 199 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Every citation record quotes its statute word for word against the pinned text under `sources/` (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
