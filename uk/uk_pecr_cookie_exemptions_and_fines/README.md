# uk/uk_pecr_cookie_exemptions_and_fines

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

UK law in force (the drafter's reading, not a lawyer's), under the 'Latest available (Revised)' texts of legislation.gov.uk fetched 2026-11-15. Three rules of the Privacy and Electronic Communications (EC Directive) Regulations 2003 (PECR, S.I. 2003/2426) as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025: (1) COOKIES: whether storing information in, or gaining access to information stored in, the terminal equipment of a subscriber or user is permitted, not_permitted or not_applicable under reg 6(1) and (2) and the six exceptions of Schedule A1 paragraphs 2 to 7 (in force from 5 February 2026), with an own comparison limb under Directive 2002/58/EC Art 5(3) and a same/differs comparison of the two answers; (2) BREACH TIME: whether a service provider's notification of a personal data breach to the Information Commission is in time under reg 5A(2) and (3A) (for a breach the provider became aware of on or after 20 August 2025), the 72 hours counted as Regulation (EU) No 611/2013 Art 2(2) and Regulation No 1182/71 Art 3 count them; (3) FINES: the tier and the statutory MAXIMUM amount, in pence, of a PECR penalty under Data Protection Act 2018 s.157 as applied by PECR Schedule 1 ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.
