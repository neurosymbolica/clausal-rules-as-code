# uk/uk_recognised_legitimate_interests

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

UK law in force, not a proposal (author's reading, not a lawyer's). Whether UK GDPR Art 6(1)(ea) read with Art 6(5) and Annex 1 (recognised legitimate interests, inserted by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 s.70 and Sch 4, in force from 5 February 2026 under S.I. 2026/82 reg 2(c) and (z6)) gives a lawful basis; whether UK GDPR Art 6(1)(f) legitimate interests does; whether either does; whether EU GDPR Art 6(1)(f) does on the same profile, set beside the UK answer (same, differs or not_comparable, with the UK provisions that make a difference); and what UK GDPR Art 21(1) gives when the data subject objects to processing based on point (e), (ea) or (f). The Art 6(1) second subparagraph bars (ea) and (f) in the UK and (f) in the EU for public authorities in the performance of their tasks. Every answer states the law as it applies from 5 February 2026, under the UK 'Latest available (Revised)' text fetched 2026-11-13 and the EU Formex consolidation 02016R0679-20160504. Out of scope: the other bases of Art 6(1) (points (a) to (e)), Art 6(3) beyond what a request states, the power to amend Annex 1, Art 21(2) to (6), the EU Art 21, the meaning of emergency under the Civil Contingencies ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.
