# uk/uk_complaints_to_the_commissioner

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

UK law in force (drafter's reading, not a lawyer's): the Data Protection Act 2018 as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 s.103, in force from 19 June 2026 (S.I. 2026/82 reg 3(a)). Decided here: (a) whether a complaint is one a data subject makes to the controller under s.164A(1) - a data subject, about personal data relating to them, considering an infringement of the UK GDPR or Part 3, made to the controller (controller_complaint_right/3); (b) whether a complaint is one a data subject may make to the Commission under s.165(2) as amended - UK GDPR or Part 3 or 4, no prior complaint to the controller required (commission_complaint_right/3); (c) the last day of the period of 30 days beginning when the controller receives a s.164A complaint within which it must acknowledge receipt (s.164A(3)), the day of receipt counted as day one and no weekend or bank holiday shift, and the transitional limit of S.I. 2026/82 reg 7 (the duty applies only to a complaint received on or after 19 June 2026) (acknowledgement_last_day/3); with a cited checklist for s.164A(1). Out of scope (deferred or excluded set): the controller's duties of s.164A(2), (4) and (5); the s.164B regulations; the ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.
