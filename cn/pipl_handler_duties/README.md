# cn/pipl_handler_duties

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

For ONE processing activity of the caller on ONE reference day: which duties the Personal Information Protection Law of the PRC (2021 adoption text, pinned npc.gov.cn page, formalised from the Chinese text) lays on a personal information handler under Chapter V (Art 51 compliance measures; Art 52(1) person in charge and Art 52(2) publishing and reporting the contact details; Art 53 dedicated body or representative of a handler outside the territory, read with Art 3(2); Art 54 compliance audit; Art 55 impact assessment and Art 56(2) keeping the report and records at least three years; Art 57 remedial measures and notices on leakage, tampering or loss, with the Art 57(2) permission not to notify individuals; Art 58 large platform obligations) and on a party processing on entrustment (Art 59), with Art 73 items (1) and (2) and the Art 74 in-force test (1 November 2021). Each answer says whether a duty is OWED, never whether it is MET; duty_contents lists what Arts 51, 56(1), 57(1) and 58 name. Open-textured judgements (handler status, the Art 3(2) purposes, significant impact, harm effectively avoided, important platform) are handed to a lawyer as needs_legal_review, never decided by ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked
