# ae/uae_pdpl_lawful_processing_and_consent

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

UAE Federal Decree by Law No. (45) of 2021 Concerning the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL), English translation from uaelegislation.gov.ae (the Arabic text prevails and was not read). For ONE processing of personal data on ONE processing date, three questions are decided: (a) pdpl_processing_lawful_basis - whether Art 4 prohibits the processing (allowed_with_consent when the consent meets the Art 1 definition of Consent and Art 6(1) and is not withdrawn under Art 6(2); else allowed_without_consent naming the first of the ten Art 4 cases that holds; else not_allowed); (b) pdpl_consent_valid - whether the consent is valid, naming the first unmet of six conditions; (c) pdpl_processing_controls - whether the Art 5 controls of items 1 to 7 are met, naming the first one failed. Every query gives not_in_force before 2 January 2022 (Art 31). Open-textured judgements (necessity, the cases, the controls, the quality of consent) are the caller's, and an unsure one is referred to a lawyer. Out of scope: Art 4 item 11 and Art 5 item 8 (Executive Regulations, not known to be issued: named gaps), Art 29 regularisation, Arts 2 and 3 (scope and Bureau exemptions), data subject rights, controller ...

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 170 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Citation records carry a label and a link to UAE Legislation portal (uaelegislation.gov.ae); no statute text is quoted and none is pinned in this folder (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
