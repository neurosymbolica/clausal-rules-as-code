> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Swiss law in force (drafter's reading, not a lawyer's). Whether a disclosure of personal data abroad is permitted under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (DSG, SR 235.1, Stand 7 July 2025) Arts 16 and 17 with the Data Protection Ordinance (DSV, SR 235.11, Stand 1 December 2025) Art 8(1), Annex 1 and Art 12, and on which route: a finding of the Federal Council (DSG Art 16(1), stated by the caller as an outside fact), one of the seven safeguard ways of DSG Art 16(2)(a)-(e) and Art 16(3) with DSV Art 12, or one of the eight derogations of DSG Art 17(1), in that cascade; whether the controller or processor must inform the Commissioner (EDOEB) on request under DSG Art 17(2); and, for comparison, the EU route under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 Arts 45(1) and 46(1), set beside the Swiss route of DSG Art 16 on the same facts. Every suitability, consent, necessity and remedy question is the caller's tri-state judgement and an unsure judgement goes to a lawyer; the acts of authorities (the Federal Council's finding, the Commissioner's approval of standard clauses, the approval of binding corporate rules, the Commission's adequacy decision) are attested outside facts referred to a lawyer ...
