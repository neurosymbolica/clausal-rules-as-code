# ch/swiss_fadp_s4_criminal_provisions

> **Disclaimer.** These rules are drafted readings derived from the cited statutes. They were generated and tested by automated processes. They are not legal advice and have not been validated by a lawyer. Where a domain lists open questions or alternative readings, they are listed in that domain. The statute text prevails: the code is subsidiary to the statutes.

## Scope

Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (DSG, SR 235.1, German text, fedlex consolidation Stand 7 July 2025) Arts 60 to 66, criminal provisions, on given facts: whether conduct falls under the one examined offence of Arts 60(1)(a), 60(1)(b) numbers 1 and 2, 60(2), 61(a) to (c), 62(1), 62(2) or 63 (fine of up to 250 000 francs); whether its prosecution needs a criminal complaint and whether one was filed; whether the five-year limitation period of Art 66 has run between two dates; and whether under Art 64(2) the authority may order the business to pay the fine (fine in consideration at most 50 000 francs, inclusive). For comparison, the ceiling of administrative fines under EU GDPR Art 83(4), (5) and (6), and whether each law provides a fine on the same facts. Out of scope (handed to the lawyer): the amount of any fine; who in a business is punishable under VStrR Arts 6 and 7 (Art 64(1)); the competent authority (Art 65); the duties the offences refer to; Swiss Criminal Code rules on intent, complaint and limitation; whether an EU fine is imposed and its amount; GDPR Art 84 penalties under Member State law.

## Where the open questions are

- `manifest.yaml`: `deferred_set` and `excluded_set` list the provisions deliberately not implemented, with the reason and the consequence for a user; `operative_readings` (where present) records readings of ambiguous text.
- `SIGNATURE.md`: the inputs the domain expects, the answers it gives, and the readings it takes.
- Answers that turn on an unsettled reading are returned as a referral for legal review rather than as a definite answer.

## What was checked

- 189 tests, passing on Clausal Prolog, Scryer Prolog and Trealla Prolog (see the repository README).
- Every citation record quotes its statute word for word against the pinned text under `sources/` (`_tools/check_citations.py`).
- This domain has no provision-tracking ledger (`provisions.yaml`): the share of the statute it covers has not been accounted unit by unit.
